## Overview

The Customer Support / Helpdesk department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED is responsible for: Resolve customer issues quickly and maintain high customer satisfaction. This department operates using MK-SupportDesk as its primary system of record for department workflows, and coordinates with other departments on cross-functional processes described in the individual SOP documents.

## Department Responsibilities

- Resolve customer-reported issues within agreed SLAs.
- Escalate confirmed bugs to Engineering with reproduction details.
- Maintain the customer-facing Knowledge Base.
- Monitor and act on customer satisfaction (CSAT) feedback.
- Flag at-risk accounts to Sales/Customer Experience leadership.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Escalating bugs to Engineering without reproduction steps or logs.
- Missing the SLA timer on Enterprise-tier tickets due to manual tracking.
- Closing tickets without confirming the customer considers the issue resolved.
