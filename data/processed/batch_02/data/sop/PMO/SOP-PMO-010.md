## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Project Manager logs into MK-PMTrack and navigates to the 'Contractor Engagement Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Project Manager selects 'New Contractor Engagement Request' and enters the required details, including scope of work, duration, and daily/monthly rate.

**Step 3:** the Project Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Project Manager submits the Contractor Engagement Request; MK-PMTrack automatically routes it to the Procurement Lead for review.

**Step 5:** The Procurement Lead reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-PMTrack updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Project Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Contractor Engagement Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-PMTrack for audit purposes. Contractor engagements beyond 6 months require re-tendering review.

## Expected Result

The contractor engagement request is completed, logged in MK-PMTrack, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-PMTrack for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Contractor engagements beyond 6 months require re-tendering review.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
