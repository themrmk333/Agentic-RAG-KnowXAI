## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **change request procedure** within the Project Management / PMO department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Project Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Change Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-PMTrack.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Project Manager**
- Approver: **PMO Lead**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-PMTrack.
- Scope/schedule/cost impact analysis must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the change request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Project Manager logs into MK-PMTrack and navigates to the 'Change Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Project Manager selects 'New Change Request' and enters the required details, including scope/schedule/cost impact analysis.

**Step 3:** the Project Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Project Manager submits the Change Request; MK-PMTrack automatically routes it to the PMO Lead for review.

**Step 5:** The PMO Lead reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-PMTrack updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Project Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Change Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-PMTrack for audit purposes. Changes impacting budget by more than 10% require sponsor re-approval.

## Expected Result

The change request is completed, logged in MK-PMTrack, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-PMTrack for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Changes impacting budget by more than 10% require sponsor re-approval.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Project Coordinator → Project Manager → PMO Lead → PMO Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-PMO-004 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
