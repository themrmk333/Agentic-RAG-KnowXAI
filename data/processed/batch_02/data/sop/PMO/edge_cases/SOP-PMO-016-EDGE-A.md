## Scenario
The designated approver (PMO Director) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the pmo onboarding cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. PMO Lead confirms the PMO Director has an active out-of-office status in MK-PMTrack or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. PMO Lead identifies the PMO Director's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Project Coordinator -> Project Manager -> PMO Lead -> PMO Director) — as recorded in MK-PMTrack.
3. PMO Lead resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-PMTrack, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original PMO Director would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the PMO Director returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-PMTrack logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to PMO Onboarding for New Project Managers decisions explicitly reserved for the PMO Director by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the PMO Director's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Project Coordinator -> Project Manager -> PMO Lead -> PMO Director.
