## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Customer Support Associate confirms eligibility and prerequisites: issue category, product area, and customer-reported severity.

**Step 2:** the Customer Support Associate raises a request in MK-SupportDesk specifying exactly which Support Ticket is required.

**Step 3:** The Support Team Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Support Ticket in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Customer Support Associate is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-SupportDesk and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-SupportDesk marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Miscategorised tickets are corrected during the daily queue review.

## Expected Result

The support ticket is completed, logged in MK-SupportDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SupportDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Miscategorised tickets are corrected during the daily queue review.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
