## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **support onboarding for new hires** within the Customer Support / Helpdesk department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Support Team Leads and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Support Onboarding** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SupportDesk.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Support Team Lead**
- Approver: **Support Manager**
- System of record: **MK-SupportDesk**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SupportDesk.
- MK-SupportDesk access, product training, and shadowing live tickets must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the support onboarding being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-SupportDesk owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the Support Team Lead prepares the required Support Onboarding, including MK-SupportDesk access, product training, and shadowing live tickets.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The Support Manager verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the Support Manager for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-SupportDesk is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New hires handle only Sev-3/4 tickets independently for the first 2 weeks.

## Expected Result

The support onboarding is completed, logged in MK-SupportDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SupportDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- New hires handle only Sev-3/4 tickets independently for the first 2 weeks.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Support Associate → Support Team Lead → Support Manager → Customer Experience Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-SUPPORT-012 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
