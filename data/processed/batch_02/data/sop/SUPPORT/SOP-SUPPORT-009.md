## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **customer onboarding support procedure** within the Customer Support / Helpdesk department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Customer Support Associates and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Customer Onboarding Session** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SupportDesk.

## Preconditions

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the customer onboarding session being requested

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Customer Support Associate confirms eligibility and prerequisites: walking the new customer through setup and key features.

**Step 2:** the Customer Support Associate raises a request in MK-SupportDesk specifying exactly which Customer Onboarding Session is required.

**Step 3:** The Support Team Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Customer Onboarding Session in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Customer Support Associate is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-SupportDesk and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-SupportDesk marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Onboarding sessions are logged in MK-CRM for the account team's visibility.

## Expected Result

The customer onboarding session is completed, logged in MK-SupportDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SupportDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Onboarding sessions are logged in MK-CRM for the account team's visibility.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Support Associate → Support Team Lead → Support Manager → Customer Experience Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-SUPPORT-009 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
