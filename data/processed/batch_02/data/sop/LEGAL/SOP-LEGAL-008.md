## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **litigation / dispute intake procedure** within the Legal & Compliance department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Senior Legal Counsels and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Dispute Case** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-LegalVault.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Senior Legal Counsel**
- Approver: **General Counsel**
- System of record: **MK-LegalVault**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-LegalVault.
- Documenting the dispute background and potential exposure must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the dispute case being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Senior Legal Counsel confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Senior Legal Counsel performs the standard first-line check: documenting the dispute background and potential exposure.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Senior Legal Counsel applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Senior Legal Counsel checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Senior Legal Counsel raises a ticket in MK-LegalVault categorised under 'Dispute Case', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Senior Legal Counsel confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-LegalVault with the root cause logged. All litigation matters are reported to the Board via Management summary.

## Expected Result

The dispute case is completed, logged in MK-LegalVault, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-LegalVault for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- All litigation matters are reported to the Board via Management summary.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Legal Associate → Contracts Specialist → Legal Manager → General Counsel. Escalations should reference the SOP-LEGAL-008 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
