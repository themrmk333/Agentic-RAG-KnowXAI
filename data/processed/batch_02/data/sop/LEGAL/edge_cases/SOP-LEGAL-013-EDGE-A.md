## Scenario
The designated approver (Legal Manager) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the brand usage clearance cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. requester confirms the Legal Manager has an active out-of-office status in MK-LegalVault or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. requester identifies the Legal Manager's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Legal Associate -> Contracts Specialist -> Legal Manager -> General Counsel) — as recorded in MK-LegalVault.
3. requester resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-LegalVault, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original Legal Manager would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the Legal Manager returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-LegalVault logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Trademark/Brand Usage Clearance Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the Legal Manager by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the Legal Manager's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Legal Associate -> Contracts Specialist -> Legal Manager -> General Counsel.
