## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-LegalVault owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the Legal Manager prepares the required Legal Onboarding, including MK-LegalVault access and confidentiality/privilege training.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The General Counsel verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the General Counsel for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-LegalVault is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. Access to active litigation files is granted on a need-to-know basis only.

The legal onboarding is completed, logged in MK-LegalVault, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-LegalVault for future reference and audit.
