## Scenario
The designated approver (CEO Office) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the crisis response cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Senior Manager confirms the CEO Office has an active out-of-office status in MK-ExecDash or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Senior Manager identifies the CEO Office's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Manager -> Director -> Vice President -> CEO Office) — as recorded in MK-ExecDash.
3. Senior Manager resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-ExecDash, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original CEO Office would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the CEO Office returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-ExecDash logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Crisis Management / Business Continuity Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the CEO Office by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the CEO Office's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Manager -> Director -> Vice President -> CEO Office.
