## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-CodeForge.

**Step 2:** the Software Engineer gathers the required data and documents, including story point estimates and dependency mapping for each backlog item.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Sprint Backlog against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The Engineering Manager discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-CodeForge with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Stories without acceptance criteria are not pulled into a sprint.
