## Scenario
The designated approver (Tech Lead) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the engineering onboarding plan cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Engineering Manager confirms the Tech Lead has an active out-of-office status in MK-CodeForge or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Engineering Manager identifies the Tech Lead's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Software Engineer -> Tech Lead -> Engineering Manager -> Engineering Director) — as recorded in MK-CodeForge.
3. Engineering Manager resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-CodeForge, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original Tech Lead would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the Tech Lead returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-CodeForge logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Engineering Onboarding Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the Tech Lead by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the Tech Lead's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Software Engineer -> Tech Lead -> Engineering Manager -> Engineering Director.
