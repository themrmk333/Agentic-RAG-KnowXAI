## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **external research collaboration procedure** within the Research & Development department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all R&D Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Collaboration Agreement** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-Labs.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **R&D Manager**
- Approver: **General Counsel**
- System of record: **MK-Labs**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-Labs.
- IP ownership terms and confidentiality provisions with the external party must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the collaboration agreement being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the R&D Manager logs into MK-Labs and navigates to the 'Collaboration Agreement' section.

**Step 2:** the R&D Manager selects 'New Collaboration Agreement' and enters the required details, including IP ownership terms and confidentiality provisions with the external party.

**Step 3:** the R&D Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the R&D Manager submits the Collaboration Agreement; MK-Labs automatically routes it to the General Counsel for review.

**Step 5:** The General Counsel reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-Labs updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the R&D Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Collaboration Agreement is processed and the record is retained in MK-Labs for audit purposes. Collaborations involving customer data require an additional Security review.

## Expected Result

The collaboration agreement is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Collaborations involving customer data require an additional Security review.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Research Associate → Research Lead → R&D Manager → R&D Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-RND-013 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
