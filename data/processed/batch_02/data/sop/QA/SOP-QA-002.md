## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-TestHub.

**Step 2:** the QA Engineer gathers the required data and documents, including requirement traceability and edge-case coverage.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Test Case Suite against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The QA Lead discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-TestHub with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Test cases without traceability to a requirement are rejected during review.

## Expected Result

The test case suite is completed, logged in MK-TestHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-TestHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Test cases without traceability to a requirement are rejected during review.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
