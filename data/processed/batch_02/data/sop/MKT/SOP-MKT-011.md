## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **vendor/agency engagement procedure** within the Marketing department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Marketing Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Agency Engagement Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-MarketHub.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Marketing Manager**
- Approver: **Marketing Director**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-MarketHub.
- Scope of work, deliverables, and budget must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the agency engagement request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Marketing Manager logs into MK-MarketHub and navigates to the 'Agency Engagement Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Marketing Manager selects 'New Agency Engagement Request' and enters the required details, including scope of work, deliverables, and budget.

**Step 3:** the Marketing Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Marketing Manager submits the Agency Engagement Request; MK-MarketHub automatically routes it to the Marketing Director for review.

**Step 5:** The Marketing Director reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-MarketHub updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Marketing Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Agency Engagement Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-MarketHub for audit purposes. Agency engagements above INR 5,00,000 require Procurement involvement.

## Expected Result

The agency engagement request is completed, logged in MK-MarketHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-MarketHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Agency engagements above INR 5,00,000 require Procurement involvement.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Marketing Executive → Marketing Team Lead → Marketing Manager → Marketing Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-MKT-011 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
