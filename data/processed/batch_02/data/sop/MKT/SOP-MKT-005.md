## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Marketing Specialist confirms eligibility and prerequisites: lead score, source campaign, and engagement history.

**Step 2:** the Marketing Specialist raises a request in MK-MarketHub specifying exactly which MQL Handover is required.

**Step 3:** The Sales Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the MQL Handover in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Marketing Specialist is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-MarketHub and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-MarketHub marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. MQLs not contacted by Sales within 24 hours are escalated to the Sales Manager.

The mql handover is completed, logged in MK-MarketHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-MarketHub for future reference and audit.
