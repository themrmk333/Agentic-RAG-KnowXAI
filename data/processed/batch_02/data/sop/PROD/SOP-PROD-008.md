## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Product Manager confirms eligibility and prerequisites: selecting beta participants and defining feedback collection cadence.

**Step 2:** the Product Manager raises a request in MK-ProductHub specifying exactly which Beta Program is required.

**Step 3:** The Product Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 7:** MK-ProductHub marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Beta features are gated behind a feature flag until GA.
