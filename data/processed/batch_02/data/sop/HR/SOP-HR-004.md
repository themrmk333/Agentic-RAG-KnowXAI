## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-HRMS.

**Step 2:** the Reporting Manager gathers the required data and documents, including self-assessment, manager assessment, and calibration discussion.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Performance Review against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The HR Business Partner discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-HRMS with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Ratings are calibrated across teams before being finalised.

## Expected Result

The performance review is completed, logged in MK-HRMS, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-HRMS for future reference and audit.
