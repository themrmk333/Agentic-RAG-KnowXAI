## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **travel expense reimbursement procedure** within the Finance & Accounting department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all employees and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Travel Expense Claim** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-Finance360.

## Applicable Roles

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-Finance360.
- Travel itinerary, per-diem entries, and original invoices/boarding passes must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the travel expense claim being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-Finance360 and navigates to the 'Travel Expense Claim' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Travel Expense Claim' and enters the required details, including travel itinerary, per-diem entries, and original invoices/boarding passes.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Travel Expense Claim; MK-Finance360 automatically routes it to the Finance Lead for review.

**Step 5:** The Finance Lead reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-Finance360 updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Travel Expense Claim is processed and the record is retained in MK-Finance360 for audit purposes. International travel claims require currency conversion at the RBI reference rate on the travel date.

## Expected Result

The travel expense claim is completed, logged in MK-Finance360, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Finance360 for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- International travel claims require currency conversion at the RBI reference rate on the travel date.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Accountant → Finance Lead → Finance Manager → Finance Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-FIN-002 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
