## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **sales onboarding procedure for new hires** within the Sales department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Sales Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Sales Onboarding** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-CRM.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Sales Manager**
- Approver: **Sales Director**
- System of record: **MK-CRM**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-CRM.
- MK-CRM access, product certification, and shadow calls with a senior AE must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the sales onboarding being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-CRM owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the Sales Manager prepares the required Sales Onboarding, including MK-CRM access, product certification, and shadow calls with a senior AE.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The Sales Director verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the Sales Director for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-CRM is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New AEs do not carry a full quota until certification is complete.

## Expected Result

The sales onboarding is completed, logged in MK-CRM, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CRM for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- New AEs do not carry a full quota until certification is complete.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Account Executive → Sales Team Lead → Sales Manager → Sales Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-SALES-010 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
