## Purpose

This policy establishes the rules and responsibilities governing **expense reimbursement policy** at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED.

## Policy Rules

1. Business expenses must be submitted in MK-Finance360 within 30 days of the expense date.
1. Claims up to INR 25,000 require only Reporting Manager approval; above that, Finance Lead approval is also required.
1. Original receipts or digital invoices are mandatory for all claims above INR 500.
1. Per-diem meal allowance for domestic travel is INR 1,200 per day; international is USD 60 per day.
1. Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval.
