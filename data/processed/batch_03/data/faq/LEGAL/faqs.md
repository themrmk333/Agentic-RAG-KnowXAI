## FAQ-LEGAL-001: How do I complete: Contract Review Request Procedure?
**Category:** Contract Management | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** requester, Senior Legal Counsel

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Contract Review Request Procedure procedure in MK-LegalVault, starting with the SOP-LEGAL-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Contract Review Request Procedure process (SOP-LEGAL-001) is owned by the Legal & Compliance department. The requester initiates the request in MK-LegalVault and Senior Legal Counsel is responsible for approval/sign-off. Contracts above INR 50,00,000 in value require General Counsel sign-off.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the requester logs into MK-LegalVault and navigates to the 'Contract Review Request' section.
2. the requester selects 'New Contract Review Request' and enters the required details, including counterparty name, contract type, and key commercial terms.
3. the requester attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.
4. the requester submits the Contract Review Request; MK-LegalVault automatically routes it to the Senior Legal Counsel for review.
5. The Senior Legal Counsel reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.
6. On approval, MK-LegalVault updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the requester by email/in-app notification.
7. The approved Contract Review Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-LegalVault for audit purposes. Contracts above INR 50,00,000 in value require General Counsel sign-off.

**Related Documents:** SOP-LEGAL-001
**Escalation Path:** Legal Associate -> Contracts Specialist -> Legal Manager -> General Counsel
