## FAQ-SUPPORT-001: How do I complete: Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization?
**Category:** Ticket Management | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Customer Support Associate, Support Team Lead

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization procedure in MK-SupportDesk, starting with the SOP-SUPPORT-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization process (SOP-SUPPORT-001) is owned by the Customer Support / Helpdesk department. The customer support associate initiates the request in MK-SupportDesk and Support Team Lead is responsible for approval/sign-off. Miscategorised tickets are corrected during the daily queue review.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Customer Support Associate confirms eligibility and prerequisites: issue category, product area, and customer-reported severity.
2. the Customer Support Associate raises a request in MK-SupportDesk specifying exactly which Support Ticket is required.
3. The Support Team Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.
4. Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Support Ticket in the relevant system.
5. the Customer Support Associate is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.
6. Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-SupportDesk and corrected before sign-off.
7. MK-SupportDesk marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Miscategorised tickets are corrected during the daily queue review.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SUPPORT-001
**Escalation Path:** Support Associate -> Support Team Lead -> Support Manager -> Customer Experience Director

The customer support associate initiates the request in MK-SupportDesk and Support Team Lead is responsible for approval/sign-off. Enterprise-tier customers are escalated automatically after 2 hours without resolution.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Customer Support Associate confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.
2. the Customer Support Associate performs the standard first-line check: reviewing the SLA timer and customer tier in MK-SupportDesk.
3. If the issue persists, the Customer Support Associate applies the documented workaround for this issue type.
4. the Customer Support Associate checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.
5. If still unresolved, the Customer Support Associate raises a ticket in MK-SupportDesk categorised under 'Ticket Escalation', attaching screenshots or error logs.
6. The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.
7. the Customer Support Associate confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-SupportDesk with the root cause logged. Enterprise-tier customers are escalated automatically after 2 hours without resolution.

Access is instead controlled per-document via the 'access_level' and 'allowed_departments' metadata, so general employee-facing knowledge remains available company-wide while restricted or management-level documents stay protected.
