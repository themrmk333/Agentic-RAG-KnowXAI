## FAQ-RND-001: How do I complete: Research Proposal Submission Procedure?
**Category:** Research Planning | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Research Associate, R&D Manager

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Research Proposal Submission Procedure procedure in MK-Labs, starting with the SOP-RND-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Research Proposal Submission Procedure process (SOP-RND-001) is owned by the Research & Development department. The research associate initiates the request in MK-Labs and R&D Manager is responsible for approval/sign-off. Proposals requiring more than 2 headcount-months require R&D Director approval.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Research Associate logs into MK-Labs and navigates to the 'Research Proposal' section.
2. the Research Associate selects 'New Research Proposal' and enters the required details, including hypothesis, expected impact, and required resources.
3. the Research Associate attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.
4. the Research Associate submits the Research Proposal; MK-Labs automatically routes it to the R&D Manager for review.
5. The R&D Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.
6. On approval, MK-Labs updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Research Associate by email/in-app notification.
7. The approved Research Proposal is processed and the record is retained in MK-Labs for audit purposes. Proposals requiring more than 2 headcount-months require R&D Director approval.

**Related Documents:** SOP-RND-001
**Escalation Path:** Research Associate -> Research Lead -> R&D Manager -> R&D Director

**Step-by-step:**

7. MK-Labs marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Experiments are never run against production customer data without anonymization.
