# Finance & Accounting — Advanced FAQs (Edge Cases, Comparisons, Policy Interpretation)

## FAQADV-FIN-001: Expense Reimbursement Procedure: What happens if Finance Lead is unavailable to approve a expense claim?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Route it to the Finance Lead's delegate (typically the next role up the escalation chain) via MK-Finance360, tagged 'Delegate Approval Required'; the Finance Lead is notified on return and can flag it for review within 5 business days if needed.

**Related Documents:** SOP-FIN-001-EDGE-A
---

## FAQADV-FIN-002: Expense Reimbursement Procedure: Can a expense claim be expedited in a genuine emergency?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Yes, via the expedited path: get verbal pre-approval from the Finance Lead directly, proceed with the minimum necessary action, then file the formal request in MK-Finance360 within 24 hours marked 'Emergency — Retroactive Approval'.

**Related Documents:** SOP-FIN-001-EDGE-B
---

## FAQADV-FIN-003: Expense Reimbursement Procedure: What do I do if MK-Finance360 is down and I need to submit a expense claim?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Non-urgent requests should wait for MK-Finance360 to be restored. For genuinely urgent cases, email the Finance Lead directly with the request details; once MK-Finance360 is back, enter the request and outcome retroactively, tagged 'Entered Retroactively — System Outage'.

**Related Documents:** SOP-FIN-001-EDGE-C
---

## FAQADV-FIN-004: Expense Reimbursement Procedure: What happens if my expense claim request is rejected, or I accidentally submit it twice?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Rejections always come with a documented reason in MK-Finance360 — correct the issue and resubmit, or escalate once if you disagree. Duplicates are flagged automatically and reconciled by keeping the original and closing the duplicate with a cross-reference.

**Related Documents:** SOP-FIN-001-EDGE-D
---

## FAQADV-FIN-005: Expense Reimbursement Procedure: What happens if my expense claim submission is missing required documentation?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** The Finance Lead returns the request in MK-Finance360 with a specific note on what's missing rather than approving or fixing it themselves; you have about 5 business days to correct and resubmit through the same request thread.

**Related Documents:** SOP-FIN-001-EDGE-E
---

## FAQADV-FIN-034: What's the difference between the standard Expense Reimbursement Procedure process and the emergency/expedited version?
**Category:** Comparison | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** The standard path (SOP-FIN-001) goes through Finance Lead via MK-Finance360 in the normal order. The expedited path (SOP-FIN-001-EDGE-B) is only for genuine emergencies: it requires verbal pre-approval first, immediate action, then retroactive formal filing within 24 hours — and repeated use of the expedited path for the same request type triggers a review.

**Related Documents:** SOP-FIN-001, SOP-FIN-001-EDGE-B
