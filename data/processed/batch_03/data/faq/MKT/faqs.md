## FAQ-MKT-001: How do I complete: Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure?
**Category:** Campaign Management | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Marketing Specialist, Marketing Manager

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure procedure in MK-MarketHub, starting with the SOP-MKT-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure process (SOP-MKT-001) is owned by the Marketing department. The marketing specialist initiates the request in MK-MarketHub and Marketing Manager is responsible for approval/sign-off. Campaigns above the quarterly discretionary budget require Marketing Director approval.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Marketing Specialist logs into MK-MarketHub and navigates to the 'Campaign Plan' section.
2. the Marketing Specialist selects 'New Campaign Plan' and enters the required details, including target audience, budget, channels, and success metrics.
3. the Marketing Specialist attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.
4. the Marketing Specialist submits the Campaign Plan; MK-MarketHub automatically routes it to the Marketing Manager for review.
5. The Marketing Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.
6. On approval, MK-MarketHub updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Marketing Specialist by email/in-app notification.
7. The approved Campaign Plan is processed and the record is retained in MK-MarketHub for audit purposes. Campaigns above the quarterly discretionary budget require Marketing Director approval.

**Related Documents:** SOP-MKT-001
**Escalation Path:** Marketing Executive -> Marketing Team Lead -> Marketing Manager -> Marketing Director
