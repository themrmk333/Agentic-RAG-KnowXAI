## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Feature adoption rate within 30 days of launch (target: above 25%)
- Roadmap delivery predictability (target: above 80% of committed items shipped)
- Customer-reported feature satisfaction (target: above 4.0 out of 5)
- Time from PRD approval to launch (target: under 90 days for standard features)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Writing a PRD without measurable success metrics, making post-launch evaluation impossible.
- Changing roadmap commitments without informing Sales and Support in advance.
- Launching features without a completed readiness checklist.
