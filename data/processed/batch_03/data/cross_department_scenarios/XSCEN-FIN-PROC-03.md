## Situation
Finance notices a recurring payment to a vendor has increased without a corresponding contract amendment on file.

## Resolution Steps

- Finance pauses the next scheduled payment pending verification.
- Procurement checks whether a contract renewal or amendment was executed and simply not filed correctly.
- If a legitimate amendment exists, Procurement files it properly and Finance resumes payment at the new amount.
- If no amendment exists, Procurement contacts the vendor to clarify and, if needed, reverts to the original contracted amount.
- Finance and Procurement agree contract amendments must be filed before, not after, a payment amount changes.

## Lesson
A payment change without a corresponding contract change is treated as a discrepancy to investigate, not a data update to accept.
