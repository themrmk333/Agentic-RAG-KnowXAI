## Situation
Finance approved a purchase for a new SaaS tool based on budget alone, without IT/Security reviewing it first.

## Resolution Steps

- IT flags the gap when the software installation request arrives without a prior Security Assessment.
- IT pauses the rollout and requests Security complete the Third-Party Vendor Security Assessment retroactively.
- Finance is informed that future software purchases should route through the Software License Procurement Procedure, which includes IT/Security sign-off, before budget approval.
- If the assessment reveals a blocker, Finance and the requester discuss whether to cancel or renegotiate the purchase.
- The Purchase Approval Policy is clarified to explicitly require IT/Security sign-off for any software purchase, not just budget approval.

## Lesson
Budget approval and security approval are two separate gates — one being satisfied doesn't imply the other.
