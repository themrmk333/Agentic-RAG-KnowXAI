## Situation
Marketing has scheduled a launch campaign, but Product's Launch Readiness Checklist isn't yet complete.

## Resolution Steps

- Product informs Marketing as soon as a readiness risk is identified, not at the scheduled launch date.
- Marketing and Product jointly assess whether the campaign date can hold with reduced scope, or must slip.
- If slipping, Marketing adjusts campaign scheduling across all channels to avoid a mismatched public message.
- If holding with reduced scope, launch materials are revised to match exactly what will actually ship.
- Both teams agree to align campaign scheduling to the Launch Readiness sign-off date going forward, not a marketing-driven date alone.

## Lesson
Public-facing launch communications should never be scheduled ahead of actual product readiness confirmation.
