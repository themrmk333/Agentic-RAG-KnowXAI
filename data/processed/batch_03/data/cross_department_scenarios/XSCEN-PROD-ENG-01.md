## Situation
Product's PRD assumes a capability that Engineering determines isn't achievable within the assumed timeline or architecture.

## Resolution Steps

- Engineering raises the feasibility concern during Backlog Grooming rather than silently descoping later.
- Product and Engineering jointly explore a reduced-scope version that's technically feasible.
- If no feasible version meets the business need, Product revisits the PRD's success metrics and priority.
- The final scope is re-documented in the PRD with Engineering's sign-off on feasibility.
- The roadmap is updated to reflect the revised scope and timeline, and Sales/Support are notified if commitments were made.

## Lesson
Feasibility concerns belong in grooming, before a roadmap commitment is made externally, not after.
