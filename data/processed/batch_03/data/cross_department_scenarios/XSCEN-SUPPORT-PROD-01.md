## Situation
Following a UI change, Support notices a sharp rise in tickets asking how to find a feature that moved.

## Resolution Steps

- Support flags the pattern to Product within the first day rather than waiting for a scheduled sync.
- Product reviews whether this was an expected adjustment period or a genuine usability miss.
- If genuine, Product considers a quick UI clarification (tooltip, banner) rather than a full redesign.
- Support's Knowledge Base is updated immediately regardless, so agents and self-service customers have an answer now.
- Product includes 'support ticket volume in the 2 weeks post-launch' as a launch health metric going forward.

## Lesson
A ticket-volume spike right after a UI change is a fast, cheap signal Product should monitor deliberately, not discover by accident.
