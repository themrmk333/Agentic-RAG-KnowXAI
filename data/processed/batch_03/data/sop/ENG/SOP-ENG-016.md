## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-CodeForge owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the departing Software Engineer prepares the required Knowledge Handover, including documenting owned services, open PRs, and pending on-call responsibilities.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The Engineering Manager verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the Engineering Manager for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-CodeForge is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. Handover documents are stored in MK-CodeForge wiki for team reference.

## Expected Result

The knowledge handover is completed, logged in MK-CodeForge, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CodeForge for future reference and audit.
