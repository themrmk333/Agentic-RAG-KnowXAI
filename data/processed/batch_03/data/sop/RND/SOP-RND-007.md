## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **compute resource request for research** within the Research & Development department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all AI Research Engineers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Compute Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-Labs.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **AI Research Engineer**
- Approver: **R&D Manager**
- System of record: **MK-Labs**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-Labs.
- GPU/TPU hours required and expected experiment duration must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the compute request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the AI Research Engineer logs into MK-Labs and navigates to the 'Compute Request' section.

**Step 2:** the AI Research Engineer selects 'New Compute Request' and enters the required details, including GPU/TPU hours required and expected experiment duration.

**Step 3:** the AI Research Engineer attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the AI Research Engineer submits the Compute Request; MK-Labs automatically routes it to the R&D Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The R&D Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-Labs updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the AI Research Engineer by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Compute Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-Labs for audit purposes. Large compute requests above the monthly quota require R&D Director approval.

## Expected Result

The compute request is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Large compute requests above the monthly quota require R&D Director approval.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Research Associate → Research Lead → R&D Manager → R&D Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-RND-007 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
