**Department:** Research & Development

## Situation
MK-Labs is down, in scheduled maintenance, or otherwise unreachable, but the ethics review genuinely cannot wait until the system is restored.

## Detection
Research Lead attempts to log into MK-Labs and receives a system-unavailable error, or has received a maintenance notification covering the relevant window (see the Communications archive).

## Decision Criteria
Research Lead first checks whether the request is time-sensitive enough to justify an off-system workaround, or whether it can simply wait for MK-Labs to be restored (the default expectation).

## Steps

1. Research Lead confirms MK-Labs is genuinely unavailable (not just a personal connectivity issue) by checking with a colleague or the IT status page.
2. For non-urgent ethics review requests, Research Lead waits for MK-Labs to be restored and submits normally — this is the default and preferred path.
3. For genuinely urgent requests, Research Lead documents the request details (who, what, why, when) in an email to the R&D Director instead of the system.
4. The R&D Director reviews the emailed request using the same criteria they would apply inside MK-Labs, and replies with an approval/rejection decision in writing.
5. Once MK-Labs is restored, Research Lead or the R&D Director re-enters the request and its outcome into MK-Labs retroactively, referencing the original email for the audit trail.
6. MK-Labs's record is tagged 'Entered Retroactively — System Outage' so reporting doesn't mistake the entry date for the actual request date.

## Responsible Role
Research Lead

## Approval
R&D Director

## Escalation
Research Associate -> Research Lead -> R&D Manager -> R&D Director

## Expected Result
The ethics review is actioned without waiting for system restoration when genuinely urgent, while still ending up fully and correctly recorded in MK-Labs once it's back online.
