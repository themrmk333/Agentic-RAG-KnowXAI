## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **crisis management / business continuity procedure** within the Management / Leadership department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Senior Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Crisis Response** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ExecDash.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Senior Manager**
- Approver: **CEO Office**
- System of record: **MK-ExecDash**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ExecDash.
- Activating the business continuity plan and notifying the crisis management team must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the crisis response being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Senior Manager confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Senior Manager performs the standard first-line check: activating the business continuity plan and notifying the crisis management team.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Senior Manager applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Senior Manager checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Senior Manager raises a ticket in MK-ExecDash categorised under 'Crisis Response', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Senior Manager confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-ExecDash with the root cause logged. A post-crisis review is conducted within 2 weeks of resolution.

## Expected Result

The crisis response is completed, logged in MK-ExecDash, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ExecDash for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- A post-crisis review is conducted within 2 weeks of resolution.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Manager → Director → Vice President → CEO Office. Escalations should reference the SOP-MGMT-010 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
