## Scenario
The designated approver (Vice President) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the team formation proposal cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Director confirms the Vice President has an active out-of-office status in MK-ExecDash or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Director identifies the Vice President's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Manager -> Director -> Vice President -> CEO Office) — as recorded in MK-ExecDash.
3. Director resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-ExecDash, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original Vice President would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the Vice President returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-ExecDash logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to New Department/Team Formation Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the Vice President by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the Vice President's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Manager -> Director -> Vice President -> CEO Office.
