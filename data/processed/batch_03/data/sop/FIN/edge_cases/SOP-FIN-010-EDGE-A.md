## Scenario
The designated approver (Finance Director) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the tax filing cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Senior Accountant confirms the Finance Director has an active out-of-office status in MK-Finance360 or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Senior Accountant identifies the Finance Director's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Accountant -> Finance Lead -> Finance Manager -> Finance Director) — as recorded in MK-Finance360.
3. Senior Accountant resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-Finance360, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original Finance Director would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the Finance Director returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-Finance360 logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Tax Compliance and Filing Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the Finance Director by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the Finance Director's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Accountant -> Finance Lead -> Finance Manager -> Finance Director.
