## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Support Team Lead confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Support Team Lead performs the standard first-line check: identifying the cause of delay and reassigning the ticket if needed.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Support Team Lead applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Support Team Lead checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Support Team Lead raises a ticket in MK-SupportDesk categorised under 'SLA Breach', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Support Team Lead confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-SupportDesk with the root cause logged. Breaches are logged and reviewed weekly for pattern analysis.

## Expected Result

The sla breach is completed, logged in MK-SupportDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SupportDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Breaches are logged and reviewed weekly for pattern analysis.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
