## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **customer complaint handling procedure** within the Customer Support / Helpdesk department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Support Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Formal Complaint** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SupportDesk.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Support Manager**
- Approver: **Customer Experience Director**
- System of record: **MK-SupportDesk**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SupportDesk.
- Acknowledging the complaint within 2 hours and assigning an owner must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the formal complaint being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Support Manager confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Support Manager performs the standard first-line check: acknowledging the complaint within 2 hours and assigning an owner.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Support Manager applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Support Manager checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Support Manager raises a ticket in MK-SupportDesk categorised under 'Formal Complaint', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Support Manager confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-SupportDesk with the root cause logged. Formal complaints are tracked separately and reported to Management monthly.

## Expected Result

The formal complaint is completed, logged in MK-SupportDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SupportDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Formal complaints are tracked separately and reported to Management monthly.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Support Associate → Support Team Lead → Support Manager → Customer Experience Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-SUPPORT-015 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
