## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **customer refund/credit request procedure** within the Customer Support / Helpdesk department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Customer Support Associates and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Refund Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SupportDesk.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Customer Support Associate**
- Approver: **Support Manager**
- System of record: **MK-SupportDesk**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SupportDesk.
- Reason for refund, amount, and account history must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the refund request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Customer Support Associate logs into MK-SupportDesk and navigates to the 'Refund Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Customer Support Associate selects 'New Refund Request' and enters the required details, including reason for refund, amount, and account history.

**Step 3:** the Customer Support Associate attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Customer Support Associate submits the Refund Request; MK-SupportDesk automatically routes it to the Support Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Support Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-SupportDesk updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Customer Support Associate by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Refund Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-SupportDesk for audit purposes. Refunds above INR 50,000 require Finance Manager approval.

## Expected Result

The refund request is completed, logged in MK-SupportDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SupportDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Refunds above INR 50,000 require Finance Manager approval.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Support Associate → Support Team Lead → Support Manager → Customer Experience Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-SUPPORT-005 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
