## Scenario
The designated approver (Marketing Director) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the marketing onboarding cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Marketing Manager confirms the Marketing Director has an active out-of-office status in MK-MarketHub or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Marketing Manager identifies the Marketing Director's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Marketing Executive -> Marketing Team Lead -> Marketing Manager -> Marketing Director) — as recorded in MK-MarketHub.
3. Marketing Manager resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-MarketHub, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original Marketing Director would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the Marketing Director returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-MarketHub logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Marketing Onboarding for New Hires decisions explicitly reserved for the Marketing Director by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the Marketing Director's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Marketing Executive -> Marketing Team Lead -> Marketing Manager -> Marketing Director.
