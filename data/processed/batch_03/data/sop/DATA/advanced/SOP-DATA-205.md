## Purpose

This advanced procedure defines how Data & Analytics handles **data & analytics after-hours emergency workflow**, covering a scenario beyond the department's routine day-to-day operations.

## Scope

Applies to on-call or available Data Analyst and Director of Data & Analytics within Data & Analytics.

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the on-call or available Data Analyst confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the on-call or available Data Analyst performs the standard first-line check: confirming the situation genuinely cannot wait until the next business day.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the on-call or available Data Analyst applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the on-call or available Data Analyst checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the on-call or available Data Analyst raises a ticket in MK-DataLake categorised under 'After-Hours Emergency Request', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the on-call or available Data Analyst confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-DataLake with the root cause logged. Non-genuine 'urgent' after-hours requests are reviewed with the requester's manager the next business day to reinforce appropriate use of this path.
