## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **employee referral program procedure** within the Human Resources department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all employees and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Referral Submission** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-HRMS.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **employee**
- Approver: **HR Executive**
- System of record: **MK-HRMS**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-HRMS.
- The candidate's resume and the open requisition ID being referred against must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the referral submission being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-HRMS and navigates to the 'Referral Submission' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Referral Submission' and enters the required details, including the candidate's resume and the open requisition ID being referred against.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Referral Submission; MK-HRMS automatically routes it to the HR Executive for review.

**Step 5:** The HR Executive reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-HRMS updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Referral Submission is processed and the record is retained in MK-HRMS for audit purposes. Referral bonus is paid only after the referred candidate completes 90 days.

## Expected Result

The referral submission is completed, logged in MK-HRMS, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-HRMS for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Referral bonus is paid only after the referred candidate completes 90 days.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: HR Executive → HR Business Partner → HR Manager → HR Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-HR-016 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
