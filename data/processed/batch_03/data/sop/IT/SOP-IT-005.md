## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **laptop replacement process** within the IT Support / Information Technology department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all employees and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Laptop Replacement** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ServiceDesk.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **employee**
- Approver: **IT Manager**
- System of record: **MK-ServiceDesk**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ServiceDesk.
- The asset tag of the current device and the reason for replacement must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the laptop replacement being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee confirms eligibility and prerequisites: the asset tag of the current device and the reason for replacement.

**Step 2:** the employee raises a request in MK-ServiceDesk specifying exactly which Laptop Replacement is required.

**Step 3:** The IT Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Laptop Replacement in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the employee is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-ServiceDesk and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-ServiceDesk marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Data from the old device is backed up to MK-Cloud before wipe and reissue.

## Expected Result

The laptop replacement is completed, logged in MK-ServiceDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ServiceDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Data from the old device is backed up to MK-Cloud before wipe and reissue.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: IT Support Engineer → IT Manager → IT Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-IT-005 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
