## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **new employee it onboarding** within the IT Support / Information Technology department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all IT Support Engineers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **IT Onboarding Kit** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ServiceDesk.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **IT Support Engineer**
- Approver: **IT Manager**
- System of record: **MK-ServiceDesk**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ServiceDesk.
- A provisioned laptop, email account, VPN profile, and required software must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the it onboarding kit being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-ServiceDesk owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the IT Support Engineer prepares the required IT Onboarding Kit, including a provisioned laptop, email account, VPN profile, and required software.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The IT Manager verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the IT Manager for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-ServiceDesk is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. IT onboarding must be completed before 9 AM on the employee's first day.

## Expected Result

The it onboarding kit is completed, logged in MK-ServiceDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ServiceDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- IT onboarding must be completed before 9 AM on the employee's first day.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: IT Support Engineer → IT Manager → IT Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-IT-007 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
