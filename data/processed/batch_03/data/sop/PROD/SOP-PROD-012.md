## Preconditions

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department

## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-ProductHub owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the Head of Product prepares the required Product Onboarding, including MK-ProductHub access, roadmap walkthrough, and customer call shadowing.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The VP Product verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the VP Product for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-ProductHub is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New PMs shadow at least 3 customer calls before owning a roadmap area.

## Expected Result

The product onboarding is completed, logged in MK-ProductHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProductHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- New PMs shadow at least 3 customer calls before owning a roadmap area.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
