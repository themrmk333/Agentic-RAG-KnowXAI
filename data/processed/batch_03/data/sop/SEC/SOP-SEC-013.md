## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **penetration test coordination** within the Cybersecurity / Information Security department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Security Engineers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Penetration Test** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SecGuard.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Security Engineer**
- Approver: **CISO**
- System of record: **MK-SecGuard**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SecGuard.
- Scope, rules of engagement, and testing window agreed with the approved vendor must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the penetration test being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-SecGuard.

**Step 2:** the Security Engineer gathers the required data and documents, including scope, rules of engagement, and testing window agreed with the approved vendor.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Penetration Test against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The CISO discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-SecGuard with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Findings rated Critical are remediated before the test report is closed.

## Expected Result

The penetration test is completed, logged in MK-SecGuard, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SecGuard for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Findings rated Critical are remediated before the test report is closed.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Security Analyst → Incident Response Lead → Security Manager → CISO. Escalations should reference the SOP-SEC-013 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
