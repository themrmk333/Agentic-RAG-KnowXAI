## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **data breach notification procedure** within the Cybersecurity / Information Security department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Incident Response Leads and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Breach Notification** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SecGuard.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Incident Response Lead**
- Approver: **CISO**
- System of record: **MK-SecGuard**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SecGuard.
- Confirming scope of affected data and impacted individuals must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the breach notification being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Incident Response Lead confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Incident Response Lead performs the standard first-line check: confirming scope of affected data and impacted individuals.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Incident Response Lead applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Incident Response Lead checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Incident Response Lead raises a ticket in MK-SecGuard categorised under 'Breach Notification', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Incident Response Lead confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-SecGuard with the root cause logged. Regulatory notification timelines are tracked jointly with Legal & Compliance.

## Expected Result

The breach notification is completed, logged in MK-SecGuard, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SecGuard for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Regulatory notification timelines are tracked jointly with Legal & Compliance.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Security Analyst → Incident Response Lead → Security Manager → CISO. Escalations should reference the SOP-SEC-016 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
