## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the brand usage clearance being requested

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the requester logs into MK-LegalVault and navigates to the 'Brand Usage Clearance' section.

**Step 2:** the requester selects 'New Brand Usage Clearance' and enters the required details, including intended use case and target market for the brand asset.

**Step 3:** the requester attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the requester submits the Brand Usage Clearance; MK-LegalVault automatically routes it to the Legal Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Legal Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-LegalVault updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the requester by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Brand Usage Clearance is processed and the record is retained in MK-LegalVault for audit purposes. Third-party trademark usage requires prior written permission.

## Expected Result

The brand usage clearance is completed, logged in MK-LegalVault, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-LegalVault for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Third-party trademark usage requires prior written permission.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
