## Decision Criteria
The General Counsel decides whether the gap is minor enough to request a quick correction, or serious enough that the request should be rejected outright and resubmitted from scratch.

5. If the same requester repeatedly submits incomplete requests (3+ times in a quarter), the General Counsel flags this to the requester's manager for a coaching conversation.
6. Once complete and accurate, the request proceeds through the standard approval procedure in SOP-LEGAL-011.
