## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Facilities Coordinator confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Facilities Coordinator performs the standard first-line check: triggering the building alarm and directing employees to assembly points.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Facilities Coordinator applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Facilities Coordinator checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Facilities Coordinator raises a ticket in MK-FacilitiesDesk categorised under 'Emergency Evacuation', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Facilities Coordinator confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-FacilitiesDesk with the root cause logged. Headcount is reconciled against the day's access-card log at the assembly point.

## Expected Result

The emergency evacuation is completed, logged in MK-FacilitiesDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-FacilitiesDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Headcount is reconciled against the day's access-card log at the assembly point.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
