## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **customer reference request procedure** within the Sales department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Account Executives and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Reference Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-CRM.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Account Executive**
- Approver: **Marketing Manager**
- System of record: **MK-CRM**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-CRM.
- The target prospect profile and requested reference customer must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the reference request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Account Executive logs into MK-CRM and navigates to the 'Reference Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Account Executive selects 'New Reference Request' and enters the required details, including the target prospect profile and requested reference customer.

**Step 3:** the Account Executive attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Account Executive submits the Reference Request; MK-CRM automatically routes it to the Marketing Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Marketing Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-CRM updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Account Executive by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Reference Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-CRM for audit purposes. Reference customers are never contacted more than once per quarter.

## Expected Result

The reference request is completed, logged in MK-CRM, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CRM for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Reference customers are never contacted more than once per quarter.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Account Executive → Sales Team Lead → Sales Manager → Sales Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-SALES-011 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
