**Department:** Sales

## Situation
MK-CRM is down, in scheduled maintenance, or otherwise unreachable, but the account plan genuinely cannot wait until the system is restored.

## Detection
Senior Account Executive attempts to log into MK-CRM and receives a system-unavailable error, or has received a maintenance notification covering the relevant window (see the Communications archive).

## Decision Criteria
Senior Account Executive first checks whether the request is time-sensitive enough to justify an off-system workaround, or whether it can simply wait for MK-CRM to be restored (the default expectation).

## Steps

1. Senior Account Executive confirms MK-CRM is genuinely unavailable (not just a personal connectivity issue) by checking with a colleague or the IT status page.
2. For non-urgent account plan requests, Senior Account Executive waits for MK-CRM to be restored and submits normally — this is the default and preferred path.
3. For genuinely urgent requests, Senior Account Executive documents the request details (who, what, why, when) in an email to the Sales Director instead of the system.
4. The Sales Director reviews the emailed request using the same criteria they would apply inside MK-CRM, and replies with an approval/rejection decision in writing.
5. Once MK-CRM is restored, Senior Account Executive or the Sales Director re-enters the request and its outcome into MK-CRM retroactively, referencing the original email for the audit trail.
6. MK-CRM's record is tagged 'Entered Retroactively — System Outage' so reporting doesn't mistake the entry date for the actual request date.

## Responsible Role
Senior Account Executive

## Approval
Sales Director

## Escalation
Account Executive -> Sales Team Lead -> Sales Manager -> Sales Director

## Expected Result
The account plan is actioned without waiting for system restoration when genuinely urgent, while still ending up fully and correctly recorded in MK-CRM once it's back online.
