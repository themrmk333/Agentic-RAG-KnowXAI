## TCK3-MGMT-00010 — Policy Governance (P3, Closed)
**Created:** 2024-06-16 via Portal  |  **Requester:** Shreya Reddy  |  **Assigned to:** Dev Menon

**Description:** Shreya Reddy (Product Management) reported an issue via Portal related to 'Company-wide Policy Approval Procedure'. Category: Policy Governance. Logged against MK-ExecDash.

**Investigation:** The assigned specialist reviewed the MK-ExecDash record, confirmed the reported symptom against 'Company-wide Policy Approval Procedure', and checked recent related changes before identifying the cause below.

**Root Cause:** Two systems that should show the same figure were pulling from data as of different points in time.

**Resolution:** Followed SOP-MGMT-009 to resolve. The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-ExecDash. the Department Manager gathers the required data and documents, including cross-functional review from Legal, HR, and the owning department. The review team examines the Company Policy Approval against the applicable policy or checklist. Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned. The CEO Office discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Verification:** A follow-up check one business day later confirmed the issue had not recurred.

**Prevention:** Added the missed check explicitly to the next review cycle's checklist with a named owner.

**Related SOP:** SOP-MGMT-009
