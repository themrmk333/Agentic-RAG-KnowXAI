## TCK3-PROC-00002 — Requisitioning (P3, Resolved)
**Created:** 2024-06-11 via Walk-in  |  **Requester:** Siddharth Iyer  |  **Assigned to:** Anjali Iyer

**Description:** Siddharth Iyer (Management / Leadership) reported an issue via Walk-in related to 'New Hardware/Equipment Request Procedure'. Category: Requisitioning. Logged against MK-ProcureX.

**Verification:** The requester confirmed the fix resolved the issue and closed the ticket satisfied.
