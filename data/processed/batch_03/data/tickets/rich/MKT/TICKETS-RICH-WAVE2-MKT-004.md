## TCK3-MKT-00116 — Campaign Management (P3, Closed)
**Created:** 2025-07-19 via Slack  |  **Requester:** Anjali Gupta  |  **Assigned to:** Preeti Kapoor

**Description:** Anjali Gupta (Legal & Compliance) reported an issue via Slack related to 'Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure'. Category: Campaign Management. Logged against MK-MarketHub.

**Investigation:** The assigned specialist reviewed the MK-MarketHub record, confirmed the reported symptom against 'Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure', and checked recent related changes before identifying the cause below.

**Root Cause:** A budget/cost-centre code entered was inactive for the current fiscal period.

**Resolution:** Followed SOP-MKT-001 to resolve. the Marketing Specialist logs into MK-MarketHub and navigates to the 'Campaign Plan' section. the Marketing Specialist selects 'New Campaign Plan' and enters the required details, including target audience, budget, channels, and success metrics. the Marketing Specialist attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting. the Marketing Specialist submits the Campaign Plan; MK-MarketHub automatically routes it to the Marketing Manager for review. The Marketing Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Verification:** A follow-up check one business day later confirmed the issue had not recurred.

**Prevention:** Added a validation check to catch this class of issue automatically before submission.

**Related SOP:** SOP-MKT-001
