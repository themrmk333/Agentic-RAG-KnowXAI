## FAQ-PROC-001: How do I complete: New Hardware/Equipment Request Procedure?
**Category:** Requisitioning | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** employee, Procurement Lead

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step New Hardware/Equipment Request Procedure procedure in MK-ProcureX, starting with the SOP-PROC-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The New Hardware/Equipment Request Procedure process (SOP-PROC-001) is owned by the Procurement / Purchasing department. The employee initiates the request in MK-ProcureX and Procurement Lead is responsible for approval/sign-off. Requests above INR 1,00,000 require Procurement Manager approval.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the employee logs into MK-ProcureX and navigates to the 'Purchase Requisition' section.
2. the employee selects 'New Purchase Requisition' and enters the required details, including item specification, quantity, and cost centre.
3. the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.
4. the employee submits the Purchase Requisition; MK-ProcureX automatically routes it to the Procurement Lead for review.
5. The Procurement Lead reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.
6. On approval, MK-ProcureX updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.
7. The approved Purchase Requisition is processed and the record is retained in MK-ProcureX for audit purposes. Requests above INR 1,00,000 require Procurement Manager approval.

**Related Documents:** SOP-PROC-001
**Escalation Path:** Procurement Executive -> Procurement Specialist -> Procurement Manager -> Procurement Director

## FAQ-PROC-010: How do I complete: Vendor Dispute Resolution Procedure?
**Category:** Vendor Management | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Procurement Specialist, Procurement Manager

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Vendor Dispute Resolution Procedure procedure in MK-ProcureX, starting with the SOP-PROC-010 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Vendor Dispute Resolution Procedure process (SOP-PROC-010) is owned by the Procurement / Purchasing department. The procurement specialist initiates the request in MK-ProcureX and Procurement Manager is responsible for approval/sign-off. Disputes unresolved after 15 days are escalated to Legal.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Procurement Specialist confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.
2. the Procurement Specialist performs the standard first-line check: documenting the discrepancy against the PO and contract terms.
3. If the issue persists, the Procurement Specialist applies the documented workaround for this issue type.
4. the Procurement Specialist checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.
5. If still unresolved, the Procurement Specialist raises a ticket in MK-ProcureX categorised under 'Vendor Dispute', attaching screenshots or error logs.
6. The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.
7. the Procurement Specialist confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-ProcureX with the root cause logged. Disputes unresolved after 15 days are escalated to Legal.

**Related Documents:** SOP-PROC-010
**Escalation Path:** Procurement Executive -> Procurement Specialist -> Procurement Manager -> Procurement Director
