## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **employee conduct / disciplinary procedure** within the Human Resources department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all HR Business Partners and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Disciplinary Case** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-HRMS.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **HR Business Partner**
- Approver: **HR Director**
- System of record: **MK-HRMS**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-HRMS.
- Documenting the incident with witness statements where relevant must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the disciplinary case being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the HR Business Partner confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the HR Business Partner performs the standard first-line check: documenting the incident with witness statements where relevant.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the HR Business Partner applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the HR Business Partner checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the HR Business Partner raises a ticket in MK-HRMS categorised under 'Disciplinary Case', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the HR Business Partner confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-HRMS with the root cause logged. Serious misconduct may result in immediate suspension pending investigation.

## Expected Result

The disciplinary case is completed, logged in MK-HRMS, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-HRMS for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Serious misconduct may result in immediate suspension pending investigation.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: HR Executive → HR Business Partner → HR Manager → HR Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-HR-013 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
