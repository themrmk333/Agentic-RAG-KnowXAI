## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Reporting Manager logs into MK-HRMS and navigates to the 'Promotion Nomination' section.

**Step 2:** the Reporting Manager selects 'New Promotion Nomination' and enters the required details, including performance history, readiness assessment, and business justification.

**Step 3:** the Reporting Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Reporting Manager submits the Promotion Nomination; MK-HRMS automatically routes it to the HR Director for review.

**Step 5:** The HR Director reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-HRMS updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Reporting Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Promotion Nomination is processed and the record is retained in MK-HRMS for audit purposes. Promotions are approved twice yearly during the promotion committee cycle.

## Expected Result

The promotion nomination is completed, logged in MK-HRMS, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-HRMS for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions
