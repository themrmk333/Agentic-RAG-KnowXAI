## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **recruitment requisition procedure** within the Human Resources department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Hiring Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Requisition** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-HRMS.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Hiring Manager**
- Approver: **HR Director**
- System of record: **MK-HRMS**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-HRMS.
- Headcount justification, budget approval, and job description must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the requisition being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Hiring Manager logs into MK-HRMS and navigates to the 'Requisition' section.

**Step 2:** the Hiring Manager selects 'New Requisition' and enters the required details, including headcount justification, budget approval, and job description.

**Step 3:** the Hiring Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Hiring Manager submits the Requisition; MK-HRMS automatically routes it to the HR Director for review.

**Step 5:** The HR Director reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-HRMS updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Hiring Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Requisition is processed and the record is retained in MK-HRMS for audit purposes. Requisitions above budgeted headcount require CFO co-approval.

## Expected Result

The requisition is completed, logged in MK-HRMS, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-HRMS for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Requisitions above budgeted headcount require CFO co-approval.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: HR Executive → HR Business Partner → HR Manager → HR Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-HR-008 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
