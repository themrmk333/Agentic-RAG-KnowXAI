## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **new hardware/equipment request procedure** within the Procurement / Purchasing department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all employees and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Purchase Requisition** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ProcureX.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **employee**
- Approver: **Procurement Lead**
- System of record: **MK-ProcureX**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ProcureX.
- Item specification, quantity, and cost centre must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the purchase requisition being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-ProcureX and navigates to the 'Purchase Requisition' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Purchase Requisition' and enters the required details, including item specification, quantity, and cost centre.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Purchase Requisition; MK-ProcureX automatically routes it to the Procurement Lead for review.

**Step 5:** The Procurement Lead reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-ProcureX updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Purchase Requisition is processed and the record is retained in MK-ProcureX for audit purposes. Requests above INR 1,00,000 require Procurement Manager approval.

## Expected Result

The purchase requisition is completed, logged in MK-ProcureX, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProcureX for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Requests above INR 1,00,000 require Procurement Manager approval.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Procurement Executive → Procurement Specialist → Procurement Manager → Procurement Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-PROC-001 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
