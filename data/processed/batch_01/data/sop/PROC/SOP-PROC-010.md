## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **vendor dispute resolution procedure** within the Procurement / Purchasing department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Procurement Specialists and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Vendor Dispute** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ProcureX.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Procurement Specialist**
- Approver: **Procurement Manager**
- System of record: **MK-ProcureX**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ProcureX.
- Documenting the discrepancy against the PO and contract terms must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the vendor dispute being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Procurement Specialist confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Procurement Specialist performs the standard first-line check: documenting the discrepancy against the PO and contract terms.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Procurement Specialist applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Procurement Specialist checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Procurement Specialist raises a ticket in MK-ProcureX categorised under 'Vendor Dispute', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Procurement Specialist confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-ProcureX with the root cause logged. Disputes unresolved after 15 days are escalated to Legal.

## Expected Result

The vendor dispute is completed, logged in MK-ProcureX, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProcureX for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Disputes unresolved after 15 days are escalated to Legal.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Procurement Executive → Procurement Specialist → Procurement Manager → Procurement Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-PROC-010 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
