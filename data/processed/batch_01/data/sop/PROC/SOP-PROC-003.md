## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **purchase order issuance procedure** within the Procurement / Purchasing department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Procurement Executives and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Purchase Order** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ProcureX.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Procurement Executive**
- Approver: **Procurement Lead**
- System of record: **MK-ProcureX**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ProcureX.
- Matching the PO to the approved requisition and negotiated price must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the purchase order being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Procurement Executive confirms eligibility and prerequisites: matching the PO to the approved requisition and negotiated price.

**Step 2:** the Procurement Executive raises a request in MK-ProcureX specifying exactly which Purchase Order is required.

**Step 3:** The Procurement Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Purchase Order in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Procurement Executive is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-ProcureX and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-ProcureX marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. POs are not issued until Finance confirms budget availability.

## Expected Result

The purchase order is completed, logged in MK-ProcureX, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProcureX for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- POs are not issued until Finance confirms budget availability.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Procurement Executive → Procurement Specialist → Procurement Manager → Procurement Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-PROC-003 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
