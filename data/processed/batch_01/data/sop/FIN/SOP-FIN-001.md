## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-Finance360 and navigates to the 'Expense Claim' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Expense Claim' and enters the required details, including expense category, amount, date, and business purpose.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Expense Claim; MK-Finance360 automatically routes it to the Finance Lead for review.

**Step 5:** The Finance Lead reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-Finance360 updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Expense Claim is processed and the record is retained in MK-Finance360 for audit purposes. Claims above INR 25,000 require an additional manager approval before Finance review.

## Expected Result

The expense claim is completed, logged in MK-Finance360, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Finance360 for future reference and audit.
