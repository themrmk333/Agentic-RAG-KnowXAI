## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **customer case study development procedure** within the Marketing department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-MarketHub.
- Customer interview notes and quantifiable results achieved must be available before starting this procedure.

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the case study being requested

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Content Strategist confirms eligibility and prerequisites: customer interview notes and quantifiable results achieved.

**Step 2:** the Content Strategist raises a request in MK-MarketHub specifying exactly which Case Study is required.

**Step 3:** The Marketing Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Case Study in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Content Strategist is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-MarketHub and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-MarketHub marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Case studies are not published without written customer approval of the final draft.

## Expected Result

The case study is completed, logged in MK-MarketHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-MarketHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Case studies are not published without written customer approval of the final draft.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
