## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Marketing Manager logs into MK-MarketHub and navigates to the 'Press Release' section.

**Step 2:** the Marketing Manager selects 'New Press Release' and enters the required details, including key messages, quotes, and distribution list.

**Step 3:** the Marketing Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Marketing Manager submits the Press Release; MK-MarketHub automatically routes it to the Marketing Director for review.

**Step 5:** The Marketing Director reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-MarketHub updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Marketing Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Press Release is processed and the record is retained in MK-MarketHub for audit purposes. All press releases require Legal and CEO Office review before release.

## Expected Result

The press release is completed, logged in MK-MarketHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-MarketHub for future reference and audit.
