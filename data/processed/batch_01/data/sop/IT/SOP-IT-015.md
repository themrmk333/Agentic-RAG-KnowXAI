## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **mobile device enrollment (mdm)** within the IT Support / Information Technology department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ServiceDesk.

- Employee ID and department

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee confirms eligibility and prerequisites: installing the MK-MDM profile and accepting the corporate device management policy.

**Step 2:** the employee raises a request in MK-ServiceDesk specifying exactly which MDM Enrollment is required.

**Step 3:** The IT Support Engineer reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the MDM Enrollment in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the employee is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-ServiceDesk and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-ServiceDesk marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Personal devices used for email must have a screen lock enabled or enrollment is rejected.

## Expected Result

The mdm enrollment is completed, logged in MK-ServiceDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ServiceDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Personal devices used for email must have a screen lock enabled or enrollment is rejected.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
