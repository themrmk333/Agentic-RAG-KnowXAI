**Step 2:** the Customer Support Associate selects 'New Severity Reclassification' and enters the required details, including business impact justification for the requested change.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-SupportDesk updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Customer Support Associate by email/in-app notification.
