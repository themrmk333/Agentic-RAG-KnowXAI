## Purpose

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Senior Support Associates and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Bug Handover** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SupportDesk.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Senior Support Associate**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SupportDesk.
- Reproduction steps, logs, and customer impact assessment must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the bug handover being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Senior Support Associate logs into MK-SupportDesk and navigates to the 'Bug Handover' section.

**Step 2:** the Senior Support Associate selects 'New Bug Handover' and enters the required details, including reproduction steps, logs, and customer impact assessment.

**Step 3:** the Senior Support Associate attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Senior Support Associate submits the Bug Handover; MK-SupportDesk automatically routes it to the Support Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Support Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-SupportDesk updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Senior Support Associate by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Bug Handover is processed and the record is retained in MK-SupportDesk for audit purposes. Handovers without reproduction steps are sent back to Support for completion.

## Expected Result

The bug handover is completed, logged in MK-SupportDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SupportDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Handovers without reproduction steps are sent back to Support for completion.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Support Associate → Support Team Lead → Support Manager → Customer Experience Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-SUPPORT-004 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
