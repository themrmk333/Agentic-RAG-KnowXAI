## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-DataLake.

**Step 2:** the Analytics Lead gathers the required data and documents, including a single agreed formula and data source per KPI to avoid conflicting numbers.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the KPI Definition against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The Director of Data & Analytics discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-DataLake with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. KPI definitions are version-controlled; changes require a company-wide notice.
