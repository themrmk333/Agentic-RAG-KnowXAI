## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **data access request procedure** within the Data & Analytics department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all requesters and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Data Access Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-DataLake.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **requester**
- Approver: **Data Manager**
- System of record: **MK-DataLake**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-DataLake.
- The specific dataset, business purpose, and required access level must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the data access request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the requester logs into MK-DataLake and navigates to the 'Data Access Request' section.

**Step 2:** the requester selects 'New Data Access Request' and enters the required details, including the specific dataset, business purpose, and required access level.

**Step 3:** the requester attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the requester submits the Data Access Request; MK-DataLake automatically routes it to the Data Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Data Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-DataLake updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the requester by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Data Access Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-DataLake for audit purposes. Access to Restricted-classified data requires Security Manager co-approval.

## Expected Result

The data access request is completed, logged in MK-DataLake, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-DataLake for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Access to Restricted-classified data requires Security Manager co-approval.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Data Analyst → Analytics Lead → Data Manager → Director of Data & Analytics. Escalations should reference the SOP-DATA-001 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
