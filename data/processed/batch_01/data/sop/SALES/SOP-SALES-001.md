## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Sales Development Representative confirms eligibility and prerequisites: budget, authority, need, and timeline (BANT) criteria.

**Step 2:** the Sales Development Representative raises a request in MK-CRM specifying exactly which Lead Qualification is required.

**Step 3:** The Sales Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Lead Qualification in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Sales Development Representative is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-CRM and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-CRM marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Unqualified leads are recycled to Marketing for further nurturing.

The lead qualification is completed, logged in MK-CRM, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CRM for future reference and audit.
