## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-CRM.
- Deal size, expected close date, and stage in MK-CRM must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the sales opportunity being requested

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Account Executive confirms eligibility and prerequisites: deal size, expected close date, and stage in MK-CRM.

**Step 2:** the Account Executive raises a request in MK-CRM specifying exactly which Sales Opportunity is required.

**Step 3:** The Sales Team Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Sales Opportunity in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Account Executive is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-CRM and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-CRM marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Opportunities without an updated stage for 30 days are flagged as stale.

## Expected Result

The sales opportunity is completed, logged in MK-CRM, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CRM for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Opportunities without an updated stage for 30 days are flagged as stale.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
