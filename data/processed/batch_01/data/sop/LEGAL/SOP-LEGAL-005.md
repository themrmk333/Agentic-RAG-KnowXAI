## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **intellectual property filing procedure** within the Legal & Compliance department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all requesters and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **IP Filing Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-LegalVault.

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-LegalVault.
- Invention/creative work description and prior art search results must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the ip filing request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the requester logs into MK-LegalVault and navigates to the 'IP Filing Request' section.

**Step 2:** the requester selects 'New IP Filing Request' and enters the required details, including invention/creative work description and prior art search results.

**Step 3:** the requester attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the requester submits the IP Filing Request; MK-LegalVault automatically routes it to the General Counsel for review.

**Step 5:** The General Counsel reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-LegalVault updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the requester by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved IP Filing Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-LegalVault for audit purposes. Filings are prioritised based on business criticality assessed by Legal.

## Expected Result

The ip filing request is completed, logged in MK-LegalVault, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-LegalVault for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Filings are prioritised based on business criticality assessed by Legal.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
