## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-FacilitiesDesk and navigates to the 'Maintenance Request' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Maintenance Request' and enters the required details, including the issue location, description, and urgency level.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Maintenance Request; MK-FacilitiesDesk automatically routes it to the Facilities Coordinator for review.

**Step 5:** The Facilities Coordinator reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-FacilitiesDesk updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Maintenance Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-FacilitiesDesk for audit purposes. Safety-related issues (electrical, fire safety) are treated as same-day priority.

## Expected Result

The maintenance request is completed, logged in MK-FacilitiesDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-FacilitiesDesk for future reference and audit.

- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
