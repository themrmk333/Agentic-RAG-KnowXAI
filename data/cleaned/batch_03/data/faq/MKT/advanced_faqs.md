# Marketing — Advanced FAQs (Edge Cases, Comparisons, Policy Interpretation)

## FAQADV-MKT-001: Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure: What happens if Marketing Manager is unavailable to approve a campaign plan?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Route it to the Marketing Manager's delegate (typically the next role up the escalation chain) via MK-MarketHub, tagged 'Delegate Approval Required'; the Marketing Manager is notified on return and can flag it for review within 5 business days if needed.

**Related Documents:** SOP-MKT-001-EDGE-A
---

## FAQADV-MKT-002: Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure: Can a campaign plan be expedited in a genuine emergency?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Yes, via the expedited path: get verbal pre-approval from the Marketing Manager directly, proceed with the minimum necessary action, then file the formal request in MK-MarketHub within 24 hours marked 'Emergency — Retroactive Approval'.

**Related Documents:** SOP-MKT-001-EDGE-B
---

## FAQADV-MKT-003: Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure: What do I do if MK-MarketHub is down and I need to submit a campaign plan?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Non-urgent requests should wait for MK-MarketHub to be restored. For genuinely urgent cases, email the Marketing Manager directly with the request details; once MK-MarketHub is back, enter the request and outcome retroactively, tagged 'Entered Retroactively — System Outage'.

**Related Documents:** SOP-MKT-001-EDGE-C
---

## FAQADV-MKT-004: Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure: What happens if my campaign plan request is rejected, or I accidentally submit it twice?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Rejections always come with a documented reason in MK-MarketHub — correct the issue and resubmit, or escalate once if you disagree. Duplicates are flagged automatically and reconciled by keeping the original and closing the duplicate with a cross-reference.

**Related Documents:** SOP-MKT-001-EDGE-D
---

## FAQADV-MKT-005: Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure: What happens if my campaign plan submission is missing required documentation?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** The Marketing Manager returns the request in MK-MarketHub with a specific note on what's missing rather than approving or fixing it themselves; you have about 5 business days to correct and resubmit through the same request thread.

**Related Documents:** SOP-MKT-001-EDGE-E
---

## FAQADV-MKT-026: If I find an old reference to the Brand and Content Approval Policy that seems to disagree with what's current, which one is right?
**Category:** Policy Interpretation | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** general

**Answer:** The current version, POL-MKT-001, is always authoritative going forward. If an older cached or printed copy of the Brand and Content Approval Policy disagrees with it, treat the current document as correct and check the POL-MKT-001-HISTORY document to understand exactly what changed and when.

**Related Documents:** POL-MKT-001, POL-MKT-001-HISTORY

## FAQADV-MKT-027: What's the difference between the standard Campaign Planning and Approval Procedure process and the emergency/expedited version?
**Category:** Comparison | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** The standard path (SOP-MKT-001) goes through Marketing Manager via MK-MarketHub in the normal order. The expedited path (SOP-MKT-001-EDGE-B) is only for genuine emergencies: it requires verbal pre-approval first, immediate action, then retroactive formal filing within 24 hours — and repeated use of the expedited path for the same request type triggers a review.

**Related Documents:** SOP-MKT-001, SOP-MKT-001-EDGE-B
