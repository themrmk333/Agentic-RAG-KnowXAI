# Customer Support / Helpdesk — Advanced FAQs (Edge Cases, Comparisons, Policy Interpretation)

## FAQADV-SUPPORT-001: Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization: What happens if Support Team Lead is unavailable to approve a support ticket?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Route it to the Support Team Lead's delegate (typically the next role up the escalation chain) via MK-SupportDesk, tagged 'Delegate Approval Required'; the Support Team Lead is notified on return and can flag it for review within 5 business days if needed.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SUPPORT-001-EDGE-A
---

## FAQADV-SUPPORT-002: Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization: Can a support ticket be expedited in a genuine emergency?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Yes, via the expedited path: get verbal pre-approval from the Support Team Lead directly, proceed with the minimum necessary action, then file the formal request in MK-SupportDesk within 24 hours marked 'Emergency — Retroactive Approval'.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SUPPORT-001-EDGE-B
---

## FAQADV-SUPPORT-003: Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization: What do I do if MK-SupportDesk is down and I need to submit a support ticket?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Non-urgent requests should wait for MK-SupportDesk to be restored. For genuinely urgent cases, email the Support Team Lead directly with the request details; once MK-SupportDesk is back, enter the request and outcome retroactively, tagged 'Entered Retroactively — System Outage'.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SUPPORT-001-EDGE-C
---

## FAQADV-SUPPORT-004: Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization: What happens if my support ticket request is rejected, or I accidentally submit it twice?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Rejections always come with a documented reason in MK-SupportDesk — correct the issue and resubmit, or escalate once if you disagree. Duplicates are flagged automatically and reconciled by keeping the original and closing the duplicate with a cross-reference.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SUPPORT-001-EDGE-D
---

## FAQADV-SUPPORT-005: Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization: What happens if my support ticket submission is missing required documentation?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** The Support Team Lead returns the request in MK-SupportDesk with a specific note on what's missing rather than approving or fixing it themselves; you have about 5 business days to correct and resubmit through the same request thread.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SUPPORT-001-EDGE-E

## FAQADV-SUPPORT-026: If I find an old reference to the Support SLA Policy that seems to disagree with what's current, which one is right?
**Category:** Policy Interpretation | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** general

**Answer:** The current version, POL-SUPPORT-001, is always authoritative going forward. If an older cached or printed copy of the Support SLA Policy disagrees with it, treat the current document as correct and check the POL-SUPPORT-001-HISTORY document to understand exactly what changed and when.

**Related Documents:** POL-SUPPORT-001, POL-SUPPORT-001-HISTORY
---

## FAQADV-SUPPORT-027: What's the difference between the standard Customer Ticket Intake and Categorization process and the emergency/expedited version?
**Category:** Comparison | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** The standard path (SOP-SUPPORT-001) goes through Support Team Lead via MK-SupportDesk in the normal order. The expedited path (SOP-SUPPORT-001-EDGE-B) is only for genuine emergencies: it requires verbal pre-approval first, immediate action, then retroactive formal filing within 24 hours — and repeated use of the expedited path for the same request type triggers a review.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SUPPORT-001, SOP-SUPPORT-001-EDGE-B
