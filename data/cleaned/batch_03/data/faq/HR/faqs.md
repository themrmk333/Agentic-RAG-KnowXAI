# Human Resources — Frequently Asked Questions (35 total)

## FAQ-HR-001: How do I complete: Leave Application Procedure?
**Category:** Leave Management | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** employee, Reporting Manager

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Leave Application Procedure procedure in MK-HRMS, starting with the SOP-HR-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Leave Application Procedure process (SOP-HR-001) is owned by the Human Resources department. The employee initiates the request in MK-HRMS and Reporting Manager is responsible for approval/sign-off. Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the employee logs into MK-HRMS and navigates to the 'Leave Application' section.
2. the employee selects 'New Leave Application' and enters the required details, including leave type, dates, and reason.
3. the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.
4. the employee submits the Leave Application; MK-HRMS automatically routes it to the Reporting Manager for review.
5. The Reporting Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.
6. On approval, MK-HRMS updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.
7. The approved Leave Application is processed and the record is retained in MK-HRMS for audit purposes. Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

**Related Documents:** SOP-HR-001
**Escalation Path:** HR Executive -> HR Business Partner -> HR Manager -> HR Director

## FAQ-HR-007: How do I complete: Employee Grievance Handling Procedure?
**Category:** Employee Relations | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** employee, HR Business Partner

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Employee Grievance Handling Procedure procedure in MK-HRMS, starting with the SOP-HR-007 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Employee Grievance Handling Procedure process (SOP-HR-007) is owned by the Human Resources department. The employee initiates the request in MK-HRMS and HR Business Partner is responsible for approval/sign-off. Grievances involving harassment are escalated to the Internal Committee within 24 hours.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the employee confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.
2. the employee performs the standard first-line check: submitting the grievance confidentially through MK-HRMS or to the HR Business Partner directly.
3. If the issue persists, the employee applies the documented workaround for this issue type.
4. the employee checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.
5. If still unresolved, the employee raises a ticket in MK-HRMS categorised under 'Grievance Case', attaching screenshots or error logs.
6. The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.
7. the employee confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-HRMS with the root cause logged. Grievances involving harassment are escalated to the Internal Committee within 24 hours.

**Related Documents:** SOP-HR-007
**Escalation Path:** HR Executive -> HR Business Partner -> HR Manager -> HR Director

## FAQ-HR-035: Can an employee from a different department ask a Human Resources question?
**Category:** Cross-Department Access | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** All Employees

**Short Answer:** Yes. Human Resources knowledge is retrievable company-wide; only documents marked restricted or management-level require extra authorization.

**Detailed Answer:** MK-Infinite-AI's knowledge base does not gate department knowledge behind the requester's own department. An employee from any department can ask a Human Resources question (for example, an IT employee asking about the leave policy). Access is instead controlled per-document via the 'access_level' and 'allowed_departments' metadata, so general employee-facing knowledge remains available company-wide while restricted or management-level documents stay protected.

**Escalation Path:** HR Executive -> HR Business Partner -> HR Manager -> HR Director
