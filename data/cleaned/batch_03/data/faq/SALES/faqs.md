## FAQ-SALES-001: How do I complete: New Lead Qualification Procedure?
**Category:** Lead Management | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Sales Development Representative, Sales Manager

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step New Lead Qualification Procedure procedure in MK-CRM, starting with the SOP-SALES-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The New Lead Qualification Procedure process (SOP-SALES-001) is owned by the Sales department. The sales development representative initiates the request in MK-CRM and Sales Manager is responsible for approval/sign-off. Unqualified leads are recycled to Marketing for further nurturing.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Sales Development Representative confirms eligibility and prerequisites: budget, authority, need, and timeline (BANT) criteria.
2. the Sales Development Representative raises a request in MK-CRM specifying exactly which Lead Qualification is required.
3. The Sales Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.
4. Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Lead Qualification in the relevant system.
5. the Sales Development Representative is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.
6. Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-CRM and corrected before sign-off.
7. MK-CRM marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Unqualified leads are recycled to Marketing for further nurturing.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SALES-001
**Escalation Path:** Account Executive -> Sales Team Lead -> Sales Manager -> Sales Director
