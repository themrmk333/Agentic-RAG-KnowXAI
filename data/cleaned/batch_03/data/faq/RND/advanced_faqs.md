# Research & Development — Advanced FAQs (Edge Cases, Comparisons, Policy Interpretation)

## FAQADV-RND-001: Research Proposal Submission Procedure: What happens if R&D Manager is unavailable to approve a research proposal?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Route it to the R&D Manager's delegate (typically the next role up the escalation chain) via MK-Labs, tagged 'Delegate Approval Required'; the R&D Manager is notified on return and can flag it for review within 5 business days if needed.

**Related Documents:** SOP-RND-001-EDGE-A
---

## FAQADV-RND-002: Research Proposal Submission Procedure: Can a research proposal be expedited in a genuine emergency?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Yes, via the expedited path: get verbal pre-approval from the R&D Manager directly, proceed with the minimum necessary action, then file the formal request in MK-Labs within 24 hours marked 'Emergency — Retroactive Approval'.

**Related Documents:** SOP-RND-001-EDGE-B
---

## FAQADV-RND-003: Research Proposal Submission Procedure: What do I do if MK-Labs is down and I need to submit a research proposal?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Non-urgent requests should wait for MK-Labs to be restored. For genuinely urgent cases, email the R&D Manager directly with the request details; once MK-Labs is back, enter the request and outcome retroactively, tagged 'Entered Retroactively — System Outage'.

**Related Documents:** SOP-RND-001-EDGE-C
---

## FAQADV-RND-004: Research Proposal Submission Procedure: What happens if my research proposal request is rejected, or I accidentally submit it twice?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** Rejections always come with a documented reason in MK-Labs — correct the issue and resubmit, or escalate once if you disagree. Duplicates are flagged automatically and reconciled by keeping the original and closing the duplicate with a cross-reference.

**Related Documents:** SOP-RND-001-EDGE-D
---

## FAQADV-RND-005: Research Proposal Submission Procedure: What happens if my research proposal submission is missing required documentation?
**Category:** Edge Case / What-If | **Difficulty:** Advanced | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** The R&D Manager returns the request in MK-Labs with a specific note on what's missing rather than approving or fixing it themselves; you have about 5 business days to correct and resubmit through the same request thread.

**Related Documents:** SOP-RND-001-EDGE-E
---

## FAQADV-RND-027: What's the difference between the standard Research Proposal Submission Procedure process and the emergency/expedited version?
**Category:** Comparison | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** employee

**Answer:** The standard path (SOP-RND-001) goes through R&D Manager via MK-Labs in the normal order. The expedited path (SOP-RND-001-EDGE-B) is only for genuine emergencies: it requires verbal pre-approval first, immediate action, then retroactive formal filing within 24 hours — and repeated use of the expedited path for the same request type triggers a review.

**Related Documents:** SOP-RND-001, SOP-RND-001-EDGE-B
