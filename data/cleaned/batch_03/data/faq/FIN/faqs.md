## FAQ-FIN-001: How do I complete: Expense Reimbursement Procedure?
**Category:** Expense Reimbursement | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** employee, Finance Lead

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Expense Reimbursement Procedure procedure in MK-Finance360, starting with the SOP-FIN-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Expense Reimbursement Procedure process (SOP-FIN-001) is owned by the Finance & Accounting department. The employee initiates the request in MK-Finance360 and Finance Lead is responsible for approval/sign-off. Claims above INR 25,000 require an additional manager approval before Finance review.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the employee logs into MK-Finance360 and navigates to the 'Expense Claim' section.
2. the employee selects 'New Expense Claim' and enters the required details, including expense category, amount, date, and business purpose.
3. the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.
4. the employee submits the Expense Claim; MK-Finance360 automatically routes it to the Finance Lead for review.
5. The Finance Lead reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.
6. On approval, MK-Finance360 updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.
7. The approved Expense Claim is processed and the record is retained in MK-Finance360 for audit purposes. Claims above INR 25,000 require an additional manager approval before Finance review.

**Related Documents:** SOP-FIN-001
**Escalation Path:** Accountant -> Finance Lead -> Finance Manager -> Finance Director
