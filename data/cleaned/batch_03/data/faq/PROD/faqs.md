## FAQ-PROD-001: How do I complete: Product Requirement Document (PRD) Creation?
**Category:** Product Planning | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Product Manager, Head of Product

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Product Requirement Document (PRD) Creation procedure in MK-ProductHub, starting with the SOP-PROD-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Product Requirement Document (PRD) Creation process (SOP-PROD-001) is owned by the Product Management department. The product manager initiates the request in MK-ProductHub and Head of Product is responsible for approval/sign-off. PRDs without measurable success metrics are sent back for revision.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Product Manager confirms eligibility and prerequisites: problem statement, target users, and success metrics.
2. the Product Manager raises a request in MK-ProductHub specifying exactly which PRD is required.
3. The Head of Product reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.
4. Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the PRD in the relevant system.
5. the Product Manager is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.
6. Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-ProductHub and corrected before sign-off.
7. MK-ProductHub marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. PRDs without measurable success metrics are sent back for revision.

**Related Documents:** SOP-PROD-001
**Escalation Path:** Associate Product Manager -> Product Manager -> Product Lead -> Head of Product
