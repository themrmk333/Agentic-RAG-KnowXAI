## FAQ-IT-001: How do I complete: Password Reset Procedure?
**Category:** Account Access | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** employee, IT Support Engineer

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Password Reset Procedure procedure in MK-ServiceDesk, starting with the SOP-IT-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Password Reset Procedure process (SOP-IT-001) is owned by the IT Support / Information Technology department. The employee initiates the request in MK-ServiceDesk and IT Support Engineer is responsible for approval/sign-off. Repeated lockouts within 24 hours trigger a mandatory identity re-verification call.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the employee confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.
2. the employee performs the standard first-line check: verifying that Caps Lock is off and trying the reset link on MK-ServiceDesk.
3. If the issue persists, the employee applies the documented workaround for this issue type.
4. the employee checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.
5. If still unresolved, the employee raises a ticket in MK-ServiceDesk categorised under 'Account Access', attaching screenshots or error logs.
6. The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.
7. the employee confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-ServiceDesk with the root cause logged. Repeated lockouts within 24 hours trigger a mandatory identity re-verification call.

**Related Documents:** SOP-IT-001
**Escalation Path:** IT Support Engineer -> IT Manager -> IT Director
