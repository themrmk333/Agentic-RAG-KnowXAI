## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **proof-of-concept (poc) evaluation procedure** within the Research & Development department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Research Leads and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **PoC Evaluation** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-Labs.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Research Lead**
- Approver: **R&D Director**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-Labs.
- Technical feasibility, cost, and business value assessment must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the poc evaluation being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-Labs.

**Step 2:** the Research Lead gathers the required data and documents, including technical feasibility, cost, and business value assessment.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the PoC Evaluation against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The R&D Director discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-Labs with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. PoCs not meeting the go/no-go criteria are archived with documented learnings.

## Expected Result

The poc evaluation is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- PoCs not meeting the go/no-go criteria are archived with documented learnings.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
