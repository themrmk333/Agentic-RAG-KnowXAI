## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-Labs owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the R&D Manager prepares the required R&D Onboarding, including MK-Labs access, compute allocation, and a literature review assignment.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The R&D Director verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the R&D Director for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-Labs is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New researchers present an initial literature review in their first two weeks.

## Expected Result

The r&d onboarding is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- New researchers present an initial literature review in their first two weeks.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
