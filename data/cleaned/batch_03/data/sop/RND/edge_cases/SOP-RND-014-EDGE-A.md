## Scenario
The designated approver (R&D Manager) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the research seminar cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Research Lead confirms the R&D Manager has an active out-of-office status in MK-Labs or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Research Lead identifies the R&D Manager's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Research Associate -> Research Lead -> R&D Manager -> R&D Director) — as recorded in MK-Labs.
3. Research Lead resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-Labs, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original R&D Manager would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the R&D Manager returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-Labs logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Internal Research Seminar Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the R&D Manager by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the R&D Manager's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Research Associate -> Research Lead -> R&D Manager -> R&D Director.
