**Related SOP:** SOP-RND-005 (Dataset Acquisition and Licensing Procedure)
**Department:** Research & Development

## Situation
MK-Labs is down, in scheduled maintenance, or otherwise unreachable, but the dataset acquisition request genuinely cannot wait until the system is restored.

## Detection
Research Associate attempts to log into MK-Labs and receives a system-unavailable error, or has received a maintenance notification covering the relevant window (see the Communications archive).

## Decision Criteria
Research Associate first checks whether the request is time-sensitive enough to justify an off-system workaround, or whether it can simply wait for MK-Labs to be restored (the default expectation).

## Steps

1. Research Associate confirms MK-Labs is genuinely unavailable (not just a personal connectivity issue) by checking with a colleague or the IT status page.
2. For non-urgent dataset acquisition request requests, Research Associate waits for MK-Labs to be restored and submits normally — this is the default and preferred path.
3. For genuinely urgent requests, Research Associate documents the request details (who, what, why, when) in an email to the Legal Manager instead of the system.
4. The Legal Manager reviews the emailed request using the same criteria they would apply inside MK-Labs, and replies with an approval/rejection decision in writing.
5. Once MK-Labs is restored, Research Associate or the Legal Manager re-enters the request and its outcome into MK-Labs retroactively, referencing the original email for the audit trail.
6. MK-Labs's record is tagged 'Entered Retroactively — System Outage' so reporting doesn't mistake the entry date for the actual request date.

## Responsible Role
Research Associate

## Approval
Legal Manager

## Escalation
Research Associate -> Research Lead -> R&D Manager -> R&D Director

## Expected Result
The dataset acquisition request is actioned without waiting for system restoration when genuinely urgent, while still ending up fully and correctly recorded in MK-Labs once it's back online.
