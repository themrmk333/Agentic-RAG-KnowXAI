## Scenario
The designated approver (Research Lead) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the model benchmark cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Senior Research Engineer confirms the Research Lead has an active out-of-office status in MK-Labs or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Senior Research Engineer identifies the Research Lead's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Research Associate -> Research Lead -> R&D Manager -> R&D Director) — as recorded in MK-Labs.
3. Senior Research Engineer resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-Labs, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original Research Lead would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the Research Lead returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-Labs logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Model Evaluation and Benchmarking Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the Research Lead by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the Research Lead's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Research Associate -> Research Lead -> R&D Manager -> R&D Director.
