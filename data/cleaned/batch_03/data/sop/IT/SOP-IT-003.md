## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **software installation request** within the IT Support / Information Technology department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ServiceDesk.
- The software name, version, and licence type required must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the software request being requested

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-ServiceDesk and navigates to the 'Software Request' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Software Request' and enters the required details, including the software name, version, and licence type required.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Software Request; MK-ServiceDesk automatically routes it to the IT Support Engineer for review.

**Step 5:** The IT Support Engineer reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-ServiceDesk updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Software Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-ServiceDesk for audit purposes. Unlicensed or unapproved software is never installed on corporate devices.

## Expected Result

The software request is completed, logged in MK-ServiceDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ServiceDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Unlicensed or unapproved software is never installed on corporate devices.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
