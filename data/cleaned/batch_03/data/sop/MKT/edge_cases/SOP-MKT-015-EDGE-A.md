## Scenario
The designated approver (Marketing Manager) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the case study cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Content Strategist confirms the Marketing Manager has an active out-of-office status in MK-MarketHub or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Content Strategist identifies the Marketing Manager's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Marketing Executive -> Marketing Team Lead -> Marketing Manager -> Marketing Director) — as recorded in MK-MarketHub.
3. Content Strategist resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-MarketHub, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original Marketing Manager would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the Marketing Manager returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-MarketHub logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Customer Case Study Development Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the Marketing Manager by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the Marketing Manager's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Marketing Executive -> Marketing Team Lead -> Marketing Manager -> Marketing Director.
