## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **content creation and review procedure** within the Marketing department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Content Strategists and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Content Piece** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-MarketHub.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Content Strategist**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-MarketHub.
- Brand voice guidelines and SEO keyword targets must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the content piece being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-MarketHub.

**Step 2:** the Content Strategist gathers the required data and documents, including brand voice guidelines and SEO keyword targets.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Content Piece against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The Marketing Manager discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-MarketHub with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Content referencing customer data requires Legal review before publishing.

## Expected Result

The content piece is completed, logged in MK-MarketHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-MarketHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Content referencing customer data requires Legal review before publishing.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
