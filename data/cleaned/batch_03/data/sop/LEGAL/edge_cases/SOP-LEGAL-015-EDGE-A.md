## Scenario
The designated approver (General Counsel) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the company policy draft cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Compliance Officer confirms the General Counsel has an active out-of-office status in MK-LegalVault or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Compliance Officer identifies the General Counsel's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Legal Associate -> Contracts Specialist -> Legal Manager -> General Counsel) — as recorded in MK-LegalVault.
3. Compliance Officer resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-LegalVault, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original General Counsel would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the General Counsel returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-LegalVault logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Policy Drafting and Approval Procedure decisions explicitly reserved for the General Counsel by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the General Counsel's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Legal Associate -> Contracts Specialist -> Legal Manager -> General Counsel.
