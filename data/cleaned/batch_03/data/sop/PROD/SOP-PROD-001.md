## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Product Manager confirms eligibility and prerequisites: problem statement, target users, and success metrics.

**Step 2:** the Product Manager raises a request in MK-ProductHub specifying exactly which PRD is required.

**Step 3:** The Head of Product reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the PRD in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Product Manager is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-ProductHub and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-ProductHub marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. PRDs without measurable success metrics are sent back for revision.

## Expected Result

The prd is completed, logged in MK-ProductHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProductHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- PRDs without measurable success metrics are sent back for revision.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
