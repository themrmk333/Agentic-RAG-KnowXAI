## Scenario
The designated approver (VP Product) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the product onboarding cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Head of Product confirms the VP Product has an active out-of-office status in MK-ProductHub or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Head of Product identifies the VP Product's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Associate Product Manager -> Product Manager -> Product Lead -> Head of Product) — as recorded in MK-ProductHub.
3. Head of Product resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-ProductHub, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original VP Product would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the VP Product returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-ProductHub logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Product Onboarding for New PMs decisions explicitly reserved for the VP Product by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the VP Product's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Associate Product Manager -> Product Manager -> Product Lead -> Head of Product.
