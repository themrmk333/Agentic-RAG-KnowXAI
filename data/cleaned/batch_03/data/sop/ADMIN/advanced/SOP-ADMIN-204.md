## Purpose

This advanced procedure defines how Administration / Facilities handles **administration / facilities dependency failure recovery procedure**, covering a scenario beyond the department's routine day-to-day operations.

## Scope

Applies to Facilities Executive and Facilities Manager within Administration / Facilities.

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Facilities Executive confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Facilities Executive performs the standard first-line check: identifying which upstream system or team Administration / Facilities depends on for the affected process.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Facilities Executive applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Facilities Executive checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Facilities Executive raises a ticket in MK-FacilitiesDesk categorised under 'Dependency Failure', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Facilities Executive confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-FacilitiesDesk with the root cause logged. If the dependency is another department, Administration / Facilities notifies that department's escalation contact directly rather than waiting silently.

## Exceptions
If the dependency is another department, Administration / Facilities notifies that department's escalation contact directly rather than waiting silently.
