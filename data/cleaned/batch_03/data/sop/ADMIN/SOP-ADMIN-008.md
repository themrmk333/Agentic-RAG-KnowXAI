**Step 4:** the employee submits the Travel Booking Request; MK-FacilitiesDesk automatically routes it to the Reporting Manager for review.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-FacilitiesDesk updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.
