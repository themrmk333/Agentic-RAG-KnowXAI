## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **internship program onboarding** within the Human Resources department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all HR Executives and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Internship Onboarding** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-HRMS.

- Primary actor: **HR Executive**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-HRMS.
- Internship agreement signature, mentor assignment, and system access request must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the internship onboarding being requested

## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-HRMS owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the HR Executive prepares the required Internship Onboarding, including internship agreement signature, mentor assignment, and system access request.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The HR Manager verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the HR Manager for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-HRMS is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. Interns without an assigned mentor within week 1 are flagged to HR.

## Expected Result

The internship onboarding is completed, logged in MK-HRMS, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-HRMS for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Interns without an assigned mentor within week 1 are flagged to HR.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
