## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Account Executive logs into MK-CRM and navigates to the 'Discount Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Account Executive selects 'New Discount Request' and enters the required details, including deal size, competitive context, and margin impact.

**Step 3:** the Account Executive attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Account Executive submits the Discount Request; MK-CRM automatically routes it to the Sales Director for review.

**Step 5:** The Sales Director reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-CRM updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Account Executive by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Discount Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-CRM for audit purposes. Discounts above 25% require Finance Director co-approval.

## Expected Result

The discount request is completed, logged in MK-CRM, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CRM for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Discounts above 25% require Finance Director co-approval.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
