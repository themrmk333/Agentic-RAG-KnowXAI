## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-PMTrack owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the PMO Lead prepares the required PMO Onboarding, including MK-PMTrack access, project templates, and shadowing an active project.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The PMO Director verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the PMO Director for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-PMTrack is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New Project Managers do not own a project solo until shadowing is complete.

## Expected Result

The pmo onboarding is completed, logged in MK-PMTrack, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-PMTrack for future reference and audit.
