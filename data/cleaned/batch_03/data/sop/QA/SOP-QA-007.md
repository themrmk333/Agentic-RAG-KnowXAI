## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-TestHub.

**Step 2:** the QA Lead gathers the required data and documents, including business scenarios and sign-off criteria agreed with stakeholders.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the UAT Cycle against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The Product Manager discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-TestHub with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. UAT sign-off must be obtained in writing via MK-PMTrack before go-live.
