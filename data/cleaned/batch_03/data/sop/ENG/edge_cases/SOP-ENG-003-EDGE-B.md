## Scenario
A genuine business emergency requires the repository to be actioned faster than the standard SOP allows, and waiting for the full standard approval chain would cause material harm (e.g., a production outage, a compliance deadline, or a safety issue).

1. Software Engineer clearly documents why the standard timeline in SOP-ENG-003 cannot be met, including the business impact of waiting.
2. Software Engineer contacts the Engineering Manager directly (not only via MK-CodeForge) — by phone or in-person — to request verbal pre-approval.
3. On verbal pre-approval, Software Engineer proceeds with the minimum necessary action to resolve the emergency, logging every action taken.

5. The Engineering Manager formally reviews and signs off on the retroactive request within 2 business days, confirming the emergency justification was reasonable.
6. If the Engineering Manager finds the emergency justification insufficient, the case is escalated to Engineering Director for a policy-compliance review.
