**Resolution:** Resolved by following the SOP-QA-001 procedure. the QA Engineer logs into MK-TestHub and navigates to the 'Defect Report' section. the QA Engineer selects 'New Defect Report' and enters the required details, including steps to reproduce, expected vs actual result, and severity. the QA Engineer attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting. the QA Engineer submits the Defect Report; MK-TestHub automatically routes it to the QA Lead for review.
