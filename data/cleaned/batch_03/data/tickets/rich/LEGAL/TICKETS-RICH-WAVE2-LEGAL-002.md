## TCK3-LEGAL-00150 — Contract Management (P2, Closed)
**Created:** 2024-09-07 via Email  |  **Requester:** Preeti Kapoor  |  **Assigned to:** Varun Iyer

**Resolution:** Followed SOP-LEGAL-001 to resolve. the requester logs into MK-LegalVault and navigates to the 'Contract Review Request' section. the requester selects 'New Contract Review Request' and enters the required details, including counterparty name, contract type, and key commercial terms. the requester attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting. the requester submits the Contract Review Request; MK-LegalVault automatically routes it to the Senior Legal Counsel for review. **Verification:** The fix was verified against the original reported symptom before closing.
