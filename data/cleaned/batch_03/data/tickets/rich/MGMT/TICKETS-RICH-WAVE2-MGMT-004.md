## TCK3-MGMT-00024 — Leadership Alignment (P3, Resolved)
**Created:** 2025-06-29 via Live Chat  |  **Requester:** Karan Bhatt  |  **Assigned to:** Siddharth Iyer

**Description:** Karan Bhatt (DevOps & Cloud) reported an issue via Live Chat related to 'Management Offsite Planning Procedure'. Category: Leadership Alignment. Logged against MK-ExecDash.

**Investigation:** The assigned specialist reviewed the MK-ExecDash record, confirmed the reported symptom against 'Management Offsite Planning Procedure', and checked recent related changes before identifying the cause below.

**Root Cause:** The target system had reached a soft capacity limit that blocked new provisioning until cleared.

**Resolution:** Followed SOP-MGMT-013 to resolve. the Senior Manager confirms eligibility and prerequisites: agenda, objectives, and pre-reading materials for attendees. the Senior Manager raises a request in MK-ExecDash specifying exactly which Management Offsite is required. The Vice President reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles. Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Management Offsite in the relevant system. the Senior Manager is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Verification:** A follow-up check one business day later confirmed the issue had not recurred.

**Prevention:** Updated the provisioning automation to fail loudly rather than silently on this condition.

**Related SOP:** SOP-MGMT-013
