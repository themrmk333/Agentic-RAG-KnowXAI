## TCK3-RND-00096 — Research Planning (P3, Closed)
**Created:** 2024-06-01 via Email  |  **Requester:** Aman Bhatt  |  **Assigned to:** Ananya Pillai

**Verification:** A follow-up check one business day later confirmed the issue had not recurred.
