## TCK3-FIN-00064 — Budgeting (P3, Resolved)
**Created:** 2024-06-05 via Walk-in  |  **Requester:** Kavya Verma  |  **Assigned to:** Alisha Rao

**Description:** Kavya Verma (Administration / Facilities) reported an issue via Walk-in related to 'Budget Approval Procedure'. Category: Budgeting. Logged against MK-Finance360.

**Investigation:** The assigned specialist reviewed the MK-Finance360 record, confirmed the reported symptom against 'Budget Approval Procedure', and checked recent related changes before identifying the cause below.

**Root Cause:** A required approval step was skipped due to a misunderstanding of the delegated authority limits.

**Resolution:** Followed SOP-FIN-006 to resolve. the Department Manager logs into MK-Finance360 and navigates to the 'Budget Proposal' section. the Department Manager selects 'New Budget Proposal' and enters the required details, including line-item breakdown and prior year actuals for comparison. the Department Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting. the Department Manager submits the Budget Proposal; MK-Finance360 automatically routes it to the Finance Director for review. The Finance Director reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Verification:** The assigned specialist re-tested the exact original scenario to confirm resolution.

**Prevention:** Added a validation check to catch this class of issue automatically before submission.

**Related SOP:** SOP-FIN-006
