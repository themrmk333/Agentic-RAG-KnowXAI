## TCK3-FIN-00127 — Financial Reporting (P2, Resolved)
**Created:** 2025-09-14 via Portal  |  **Requester:** Yash Malhotra  |  **Assigned to:** Kavya Nair

**Description:** Yash Malhotra (Management / Leadership) reported an issue via Portal related to 'Monthly Financial Closing Procedure'. Category: Financial Reporting. Logged against MK-Finance360.

**Investigation:** The assigned specialist reviewed the MK-Finance360 record, confirmed the reported symptom against 'Monthly Financial Closing Procedure', and checked recent related changes before identifying the cause below.

**Root Cause:** A recent policy change hadn't been fully propagated to every downstream checklist yet.

**Resolution:** Followed SOP-FIN-007 to resolve. The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-Finance360. the Senior Accountant gathers the required data and documents, including reconciling all bank accounts, accruals, and intercompany balances. The review team examines the Month-End Close against the applicable policy or checklist. Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned. The Finance Manager discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Verification:** A follow-up check one business day later confirmed the issue had not recurred.

**Prevention:** Added the missed check explicitly to the next review cycle's checklist with a named owner.

**Related SOP:** SOP-FIN-007
