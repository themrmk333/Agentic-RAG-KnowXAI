## TCK3-MKT-00133 — Content Marketing (P4, Closed)
**Created:** 2025-01-29 via Live Chat  |  **Requester:** Neel Sharma  |  **Assigned to:** Harsh Joshi

**Description:** Neel Sharma (Software Engineering / Development) reported an issue via Live Chat related to 'Content Creation and Review Procedure'. Category: Content Marketing. Logged against MK-MarketHub.

**Investigation:** The assigned specialist reviewed the MK-MarketHub record, confirmed the reported symptom against 'Content Creation and Review Procedure', and checked recent related changes before identifying the cause below.

**Root Cause:** Two systems that should show the same figure were pulling from data as of different points in time.

**Resolution:** Followed SOP-MKT-002 to resolve. The Marketing Manager discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Verification:** The fix was verified against the original reported symptom before closing.

**Prevention:** Added the missed check explicitly to the next review cycle's checklist with a named owner.

**Related SOP:** SOP-MKT-002
