## TCK3-MKT-00065 — Onboarding (P3, Resolved)
**Created:** 2025-12-21 via Portal  |  **Requester:** Sneha Singh  |  **Assigned to:** Gaurav Chowdhury

**Description:** Sneha Singh (Customer Support / Helpdesk) reported an issue via Portal related to 'Marketing Onboarding for New Hires'. Category: Onboarding. Logged against MK-MarketHub.

**Investigation:** The assigned specialist reviewed the MK-MarketHub record, confirmed the reported symptom against 'Marketing Onboarding for New Hires', and checked recent related changes before identifying the cause below.

**Root Cause:** The handover checklist used was an older version missing a step added since.

**Resolution:** Followed SOP-MKT-013 to resolve. HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-MarketHub owner ahead of the employee's start or end date. the Marketing Manager prepares the required Marketing Onboarding, including MK-MarketHub access, brand guideline walkthrough, and channel ownership handover. Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding). The Marketing Director verifies that every item on the checklist is complete. The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Verification:** The assigned specialist re-tested the exact original scenario to confirm resolution.

**Prevention:** Updated the handover/checklist template to make the missed step explicit and mandatory.

**Related SOP:** SOP-MKT-013
