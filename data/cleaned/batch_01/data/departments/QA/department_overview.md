## Overview

The QA / Testing department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED is responsible for: Protect product quality through rigorous, repeatable testing before every release. This department operates using MK-TestHub as its primary system of record for department workflows, and coordinates with other departments on cross-functional processes described in the individual SOP documents.

## Department Responsibilities

- Design and execute test plans covering functional, regression, and performance testing.
- Report, triage, and track software defects to resolution.
- Provide release readiness sign-off before production deployment.
- Build and maintain the automated test suite.
- Coordinate user acceptance testing with business stakeholders.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Defect leakage rate to production (target: under 3%)
- Test automation coverage (target: above 60% of regression suite)
- Average defect turnaround time (target: under 3 days for Sev-2)
- Release sign-off on-time rate (target: above 95%)

| Flaky Test | An automated test that passes/fails inconsistently without code changes. |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Logging defects without clear reproduction steps, causing rework during triage.
- Skipping regression testing on 'small' changes that later cause production issues.
- Not tagging defect severity accurately, causing Sev-1 issues to be missed in triage.
