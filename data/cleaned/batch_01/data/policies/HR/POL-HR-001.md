## Policy Rules

1. 5 Casual Leave days per year are provided for short-notice personal matters.
1. Unused Paid Annual Leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next calendar year; the remainder lapses on 31 December.

1. Leave without pay (LWP) may be granted by the HR Director in exceptional circumstances for up to 30 days.
