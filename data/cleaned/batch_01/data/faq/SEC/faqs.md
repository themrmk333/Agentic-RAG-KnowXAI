## FAQ-SEC-001: How do I complete: Phishing Email Reporting Procedure?
**Category:** Phishing | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** employee, Security Analyst

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Phishing Email Reporting Procedure procedure in MK-SecGuard, starting with the SOP-SEC-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Phishing Email Reporting Procedure process (SOP-SEC-001) is owned by the Cybersecurity / Information Security department. The employee initiates the request in MK-SecGuard and Security Analyst is responsible for approval/sign-off. Confirmed phishing campaigns trigger a company-wide alert within 1 hour.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the employee confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.
2. the employee performs the standard first-line check: not clicking any links or replying, and using the 'Report Phishing' button in the email client.
3. If the issue persists, the employee applies the documented workaround for this issue type.
4. the employee checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.
5. If still unresolved, the employee raises a ticket in MK-SecGuard categorised under 'Phishing Report', attaching screenshots or error logs.
6. The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.
7. the employee confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-SecGuard with the root cause logged. Confirmed phishing campaigns trigger a company-wide alert within 1 hour.

**Related Documents:** SOP-SEC-001
**Escalation Path:** Security Analyst -> Incident Response Lead -> Security Manager -> CISO
