## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-TestHub owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the QA Lead prepares the required QA Onboarding Plan, including MK-TestHub access, sample test suite walkthrough, and an onboarding buddy.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The QA Manager verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the QA Manager for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-TestHub is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New QA hires shadow a full regression cycle in week one.

## Expected Result

The qa onboarding plan is completed, logged in MK-TestHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-TestHub for future reference and audit.
