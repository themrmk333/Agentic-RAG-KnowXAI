## Required Information

- Employee ID and department

## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-CodeForge owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the Engineering Manager prepares the required Engineering Onboarding Plan, including repository access, MK-CodeForge SSH keys, and assigning an onboarding buddy.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The Tech Lead verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the Tech Lead for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-CodeForge is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New engineers ship their first small PR within the first week wherever possible.

## Expected Result

The engineering onboarding plan is completed, logged in MK-CodeForge, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CodeForge for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- New engineers ship their first small PR within the first week wherever possible.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Software Engineer → Tech Lead → Engineering Manager → Engineering Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-ENG-012 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
