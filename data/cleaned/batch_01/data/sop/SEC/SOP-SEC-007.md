## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **new employee security onboarding** within the Cybersecurity / Information Security department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Security Analysts and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Security Onboarding** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SecGuard.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Security Analyst**
- Approver: **Security Manager**
- System of record: **MK-SecGuard**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SecGuard.
- MFA enrollment, security awareness training assignment, and acceptable use acknowledgment must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the security onboarding being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-SecGuard owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the Security Analyst prepares the required Security Onboarding, including MFA enrollment, security awareness training assignment, and acceptable use acknowledgment.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The Security Manager verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the Security Manager for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-SecGuard is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. Employees cannot access production systems until security onboarding is complete.

## Expected Result

The security onboarding is completed, logged in MK-SecGuard, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SecGuard for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Employees cannot access production systems until security onboarding is complete.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Security Analyst → Incident Response Lead → Security Manager → CISO. Escalations should reference the SOP-SEC-007 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
