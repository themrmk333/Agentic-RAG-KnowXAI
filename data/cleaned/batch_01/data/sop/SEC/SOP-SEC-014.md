## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **privileged access management (pam) request** within the Cybersecurity / Information Security department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all requesters and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Privileged Access Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SecGuard.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **requester**
- Approver: **Security Manager**
- System of record: **MK-SecGuard**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SecGuard.
- The specific system, duration, and business justification for elevated access must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the privileged access request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the requester logs into MK-SecGuard and navigates to the 'Privileged Access Request' section.

**Step 2:** the requester selects 'New Privileged Access Request' and enters the required details, including the specific system, duration, and business justification for elevated access.

**Step 3:** the requester attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the requester submits the Privileged Access Request; MK-SecGuard automatically routes it to the Security Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Security Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-SecGuard updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the requester by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Privileged Access Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-SecGuard for audit purposes. Privileged sessions are recorded and reviewed monthly.

## Expected Result

The privileged access request is completed, logged in MK-SecGuard, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SecGuard for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Privileged sessions are recorded and reviewed monthly.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Security Analyst → Incident Response Lead → Security Manager → CISO. Escalations should reference the SOP-SEC-014 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
