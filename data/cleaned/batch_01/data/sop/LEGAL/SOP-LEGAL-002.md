## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Legal Associate confirms eligibility and prerequisites: using the standard MK-Infinite-AI mutual NDA template where possible.

**Step 2:** the Legal Associate raises a request in MK-LegalVault specifying exactly which Non-Disclosure Agreement is required.

**Step 3:** The Legal Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Non-Disclosure Agreement in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Legal Associate is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-LegalVault and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-LegalVault marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Non-standard NDA terms require Senior Legal Counsel review before signature.

## Expected Result

The non-disclosure agreement is completed, logged in MK-LegalVault, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-LegalVault for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Non-standard NDA terms require Senior Legal Counsel review before signature.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
