## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **whistleblower complaint handling procedure** within the Legal & Compliance department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Compliance Officers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Whistleblower Case** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-LegalVault.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Compliance Officer**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-LegalVault.
- Receiving the complaint confidentially through the designated channel must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the whistleblower case being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Compliance Officer confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Compliance Officer performs the standard first-line check: receiving the complaint confidentially through the designated channel.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Compliance Officer applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Compliance Officer checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Compliance Officer raises a ticket in MK-LegalVault categorised under 'Whistleblower Case', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Compliance Officer confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-LegalVault with the root cause logged. Retaliation against a whistleblower is treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

## Expected Result

The whistleblower case is completed, logged in MK-LegalVault, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-LegalVault for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Retaliation against a whistleblower is treated as a serious disciplinary matter.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Legal Associate → Contracts Specialist → Legal Manager → General Counsel. Escalations should reference the SOP-LEGAL-012 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
