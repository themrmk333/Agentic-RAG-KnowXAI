## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **feature deprecation procedure** within the Product Management department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Product Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Deprecation Plan** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ProductHub.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Product Manager**
- Approver: **Head of Product**
- System of record: **MK-ProductHub**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ProductHub.
- Usage data, customer notice period, and migration path must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the deprecation plan being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Product Manager logs into MK-ProductHub and navigates to the 'Deprecation Plan' section.

**Step 2:** the Product Manager selects 'New Deprecation Plan' and enters the required details, including usage data, customer notice period, and migration path.

**Step 3:** the Product Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Product Manager submits the Deprecation Plan; MK-ProductHub automatically routes it to the Head of Product for review.

**Step 5:** The Head of Product reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-ProductHub updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Product Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Deprecation Plan is processed and the record is retained in MK-ProductHub for audit purposes. Deprecations affecting paying customers require 90 days' notice.

## Expected Result

The deprecation plan is completed, logged in MK-ProductHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProductHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Deprecations affecting paying customers require 90 days' notice.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Associate Product Manager → Product Manager → Product Lead → Head of Product. Escalations should reference the SOP-PROD-013 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
