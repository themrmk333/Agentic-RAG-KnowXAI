## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-ProductHub.

**Step 2:** the Product Manager gathers the required data and documents, including adoption, retention, and engagement metrics from MK-DataLake dashboards.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Metrics Review against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The Head of Product discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-ProductHub with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Metrics declining two months in a row trigger a root-cause deep dive.

## Expected Result

The metrics review is completed, logged in MK-ProductHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProductHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Metrics declining two months in a row trigger a root-cause deep dive.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
