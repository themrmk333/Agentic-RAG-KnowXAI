## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-ProcureX.

**Step 2:** the Procurement Specialist gathers the required data and documents, including comparing at least 3 quotes on price, quality, and delivery timeline.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Vendor Selection against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The Procurement Manager discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-ProcureX with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Sole-sourcing without competitive quotes requires Director approval with justification.

## Expected Result

The vendor selection is completed, logged in MK-ProcureX, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProcureX for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Sole-sourcing without competitive quotes requires Director approval with justification.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
