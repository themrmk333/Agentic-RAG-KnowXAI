## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Procurement Executive confirms eligibility and prerequisites: checking quantity and condition against the purchase order.

**Step 2:** the Procurement Executive raises a request in MK-ProcureX specifying exactly which Goods Receipt is required.

**Step 3:** The Procurement Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Goods Receipt in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Procurement Executive is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-ProcureX and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-ProcureX marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Damaged or short-shipped goods trigger an immediate vendor claim.

The goods receipt is completed, logged in MK-ProcureX, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProcureX for future reference and audit.

- Damaged or short-shipped goods trigger an immediate vendor claim.
