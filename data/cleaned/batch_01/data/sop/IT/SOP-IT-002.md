## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **new hardware request fulfillment** within the IT Support / Information Technology department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all employees and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Hardware Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ServiceDesk.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **employee**
- Approver: **IT Manager**
- System of record: **MK-ServiceDesk**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ServiceDesk.
- Device type, business justification, and cost centre must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the hardware request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-ServiceDesk and navigates to the 'Hardware Request' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Hardware Request' and enters the required details, including device type, business justification, and cost centre.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Hardware Request; MK-ServiceDesk automatically routes it to the IT Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The IT Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-ServiceDesk updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Hardware Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-ServiceDesk for audit purposes. Requests above INR 80,000 require additional Finance sign-off.

## Expected Result

The hardware request is completed, logged in MK-ServiceDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ServiceDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Requests above INR 80,000 require additional Finance sign-off.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: IT Support Engineer → IT Manager → IT Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-IT-002 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
