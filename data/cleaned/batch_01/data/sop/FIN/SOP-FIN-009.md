## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Finance Executive confirms eligibility and prerequisites: the receipt and purpose for each petty cash disbursement.

**Step 2:** the Finance Executive raises a request in MK-Finance360 specifying exactly which Petty Cash Voucher is required.

**Step 3:** The Finance Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Petty Cash Voucher in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Finance Executive is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-Finance360 and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-Finance360 marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Petty cash float is capped at INR 25,000 per office location.

The petty cash voucher is completed, logged in MK-Finance360, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Finance360 for future reference and audit.
