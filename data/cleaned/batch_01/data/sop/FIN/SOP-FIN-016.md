## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **salary advance request procedure** within the Finance & Accounting department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all employees and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Salary Advance Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-Finance360.

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-Finance360.
- Reason for advance and requested repayment schedule must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the salary advance request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-Finance360 and navigates to the 'Salary Advance Request' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Salary Advance Request' and enters the required details, including reason for advance and requested repayment schedule.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Salary Advance Request; MK-Finance360 automatically routes it to the HR Business Partner for review.

**Step 5:** The HR Business Partner reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-Finance360 updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Salary Advance Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-Finance360 for audit purposes. Advances are capped at one month's net salary and repaid over up to 3 months.

## Expected Result

The salary advance request is completed, logged in MK-Finance360, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Finance360 for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Advances are capped at one month's net salary and repaid over up to 3 months.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
