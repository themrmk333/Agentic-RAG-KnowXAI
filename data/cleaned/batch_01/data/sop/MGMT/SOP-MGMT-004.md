## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Director confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.

**Step 2:** the Director performs the standard first-line check: summarising the decision required, options considered, and recommendation.

**Step 3:** If the issue persists, the Director applies the documented workaround for this issue type.

**Step 4:** the Director checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.

**Step 5:** If still unresolved, the Director raises a ticket in MK-ExecDash categorised under 'Executive Escalation', attaching screenshots or error logs.

**Step 6:** The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.

**Step 7:** the Director confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-ExecDash with the root cause logged. Time-critical escalations are addressed within 24 hours via the CEO Office.
