## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Research Engineer logs into MK-Labs and navigates to the 'Publication Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Research Engineer selects 'New Publication Request' and enters the required details, including checking for any confidential or IP-sensitive content before submission.

**Step 3:** the Research Engineer attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Research Engineer submits the Publication Request; MK-Labs automatically routes it to the R&D Director for review.

**Step 5:** The R&D Director reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-Labs updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Research Engineer by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Publication Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-Labs for audit purposes. External publications require Legal review for IP protection ahead of submission.

The publication request is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.
