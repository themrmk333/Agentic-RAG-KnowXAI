## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **internal research seminar procedure** within the Research & Development department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Research Leads and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Research Seminar** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-Labs.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Research Lead**
- Approver: **R&D Manager**
- System of record: **MK-Labs**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-Labs.
- Scheduling a monthly seminar slot and circulating pre-read material must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the research seminar being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Research Lead confirms eligibility and prerequisites: scheduling a monthly seminar slot and circulating pre-read material.

**Step 2:** the Research Lead raises a request in MK-Labs specifying exactly which Research Seminar is required.

**Step 3:** The R&D Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Research Seminar in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Research Lead is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-Labs and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-Labs marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Seminar recordings are stored in MK-Labs for employees who could not attend.

## Expected Result

The research seminar is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Seminar recordings are stored in MK-Labs for employees who could not attend.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
