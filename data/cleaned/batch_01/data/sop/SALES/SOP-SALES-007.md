## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Account Executive logs into MK-CRM and navigates to the 'Renewal Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Account Executive selects 'New Renewal Request' and enters the required details, including usage history, satisfaction indicators, and proposed renewal terms.

**Step 3:** the Account Executive attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Account Executive submits the Renewal Request; MK-CRM automatically routes it to the Sales Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Sales Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-CRM updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Account Executive by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Renewal Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-CRM for audit purposes. At-risk renewals are flagged to the Customer Experience Director 90 days ahead.

## Expected Result

The renewal request is completed, logged in MK-CRM, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CRM for future reference and audit.

- At-risk renewals are flagged to the Customer Experience Director 90 days ahead.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
