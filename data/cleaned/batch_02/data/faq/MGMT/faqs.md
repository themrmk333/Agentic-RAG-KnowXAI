## FAQ-MGMT-001: How do I complete: Company Strategy Planning Procedure?
**Category:** Strategic Planning | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Department Director, CEO Office

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Company Strategy Planning Procedure procedure in MK-ExecDash, starting with the SOP-MGMT-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Company Strategy Planning Procedure process (SOP-MGMT-001) is owned by the Management / Leadership department. The department director initiates the request in MK-ExecDash and CEO Office is responsible for approval/sign-off. Strategy is reviewed and refreshed annually with quarterly checkpoints.

**Step-by-step:**
1. The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-ExecDash.
2. the Department Director gathers the required data and documents, including market analysis, competitive positioning, and 3-year objectives.
3. The review team examines the Strategic Plan against the applicable policy or checklist.
4. Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.
5. The CEO Office discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.
6. Remediation actions are tracked in MK-ExecDash with due dates and status.
7. The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Strategy is reviewed and refreshed annually with quarterly checkpoints.

**Related Documents:** SOP-MGMT-001
**Escalation Path:** Manager -> Director -> Vice President -> CEO Office

**Step-by-step:**

2. the Director performs the standard first-line check: summarising the decision required, options considered, and recommendation.
3. If the issue persists, the Director applies the documented workaround for this issue type.
4. the Director checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.
5. If still unresolved, the Director raises a ticket in MK-ExecDash categorised under 'Executive Escalation', attaching screenshots or error logs.
6. The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.
7. the Director confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-ExecDash with the root cause logged. Time-critical escalations are addressed within 24 hours via the CEO Office.
