## FAQ-ADMIN-001: How do I complete: New Employee Workspace Setup Procedure?
**Category:** Facilities | **Difficulty:** Basic | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Facilities Executive, Office Manager

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step New Employee Workspace Setup Procedure procedure in MK-FacilitiesDesk, starting with the SOP-ADMIN-001 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The New Employee Workspace Setup Procedure process (SOP-ADMIN-001) is owned by the Administration / Facilities department. The facilities executive initiates the request in MK-FacilitiesDesk and Office Manager is responsible for approval/sign-off. Workspace setup is completed a day before the employee's first working day.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Facilities Executive confirms eligibility and prerequisites: desk allocation, access card, and welcome kit preparation.
2. the Facilities Executive raises a request in MK-FacilitiesDesk specifying exactly which Workspace Setup is required.
3. The Office Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.
4. Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Workspace Setup in the relevant system.
5. the Facilities Executive is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.
6. Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-FacilitiesDesk and corrected before sign-off.
7. MK-FacilitiesDesk marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Workspace setup is completed a day before the employee's first working day.

**Related Documents:** SOP-ADMIN-001
**Escalation Path:** Facilities Executive -> Facilities Coordinator -> Facilities Manager -> Head of Administration

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Office Safety Drill Procedure procedure in MK-FacilitiesDesk, starting with the SOP-ADMIN-007 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Office Safety Drill Procedure process (SOP-ADMIN-007) is owned by the Administration / Facilities department. The facilities manager initiates the request in MK-FacilitiesDesk and Head of Administration is responsible for approval/sign-off. Drills are conducted twice yearly per office location.

**Step-by-step:**
1. The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-FacilitiesDesk.
2. the Facilities Manager gathers the required data and documents, including evacuation time, headcount reconciliation, and identified gaps.
3. The review team examines the Safety Drill Report against the applicable policy or checklist.
4. Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.
5. The Head of Administration discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.
6. Remediation actions are tracked in MK-FacilitiesDesk with due dates and status.
7. The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Drills are conducted twice yearly per office location.

## FAQ-ADMIN-013: How do I complete: Emergency Evacuation Procedure?
**Category:** Health & Safety | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** Facilities Coordinator, Head of Administration

**Short Answer:** Follow the 7-step Emergency Evacuation Procedure procedure in MK-FacilitiesDesk, starting with the SOP-ADMIN-013 SOP.

**Detailed Answer:** The Emergency Evacuation Procedure process (SOP-ADMIN-013) is owned by the Administration / Facilities department. The facilities coordinator initiates the request in MK-FacilitiesDesk and Head of Administration is responsible for approval/sign-off. Headcount is reconciled against the day's access-card log at the assembly point.

**Step-by-step:**
1. the Facilities Coordinator confirms the exact symptom, when it started, and whether it affects one system or several.
2. the Facilities Coordinator performs the standard first-line check: triggering the building alarm and directing employees to assembly points.
3. If the issue persists, the Facilities Coordinator applies the documented workaround for this issue type.
4. the Facilities Coordinator checks whether the issue is isolated to their own account/device or is affecting other users too.
5. If still unresolved, the Facilities Coordinator raises a ticket in MK-FacilitiesDesk categorised under 'Emergency Evacuation', attaching screenshots or error logs.
6. The assigned specialist reproduces the issue, diagnoses the root cause, and applies a fix or a temporary workaround.
7. the Facilities Coordinator confirms the fix works; the ticket is closed in MK-FacilitiesDesk with the root cause logged. Headcount is reconciled against the day's access-card log at the assembly point.

**Related Documents:** SOP-ADMIN-013
**Escalation Path:** Facilities Executive -> Facilities Coordinator -> Facilities Manager -> Head of Administration

**Category:** Cross-Department Access | **Difficulty:** Intermediate | **Access Level:** general | **Roles:** All Employees

**Short Answer:** Yes. Administration / Facilities knowledge is retrievable company-wide; only documents marked restricted or management-level require extra authorization.

**Detailed Answer:** MK-Infinite-AI's knowledge base does not gate department knowledge behind the requester's own department. An employee from any department can ask a Administration / Facilities question (for example, an IT employee asking about the leave policy). Access is instead controlled per-document via the 'access_level' and 'allowed_departments' metadata, so general employee-facing knowledge remains available company-wide while restricted or management-level documents stay protected.

**Escalation Path:** Facilities Executive -> Facilities Coordinator -> Facilities Manager -> Head of Administration
