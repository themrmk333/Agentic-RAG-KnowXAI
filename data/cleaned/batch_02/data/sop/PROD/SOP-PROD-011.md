This SOP defines the standard procedure for **cross-functional product sync procedure** within the Product Management department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

This procedure applies to all Product Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Product Sync Meeting** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-ProductHub.

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-ProductHub.
- Aligning Engineering, Design, and QA on the sprint goal must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Product Manager confirms eligibility and prerequisites: aligning Engineering, Design, and QA on the sprint goal.

**Step 2:** the Product Manager raises a request in MK-ProductHub specifying exactly which Product Sync Meeting is required.

**Step 3:** The Product Lead reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Product Sync Meeting in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Product Manager is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-ProductHub and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-ProductHub marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Unresolved cross-team dependencies are escalated to the PMO.

## Expected Result

The product sync meeting is completed, logged in MK-ProductHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProductHub for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Unresolved cross-team dependencies are escalated to the PMO.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
