## Scenario
A genuine business emergency requires the product onboarding to be actioned faster than the standard SOP allows, and waiting for the full standard approval chain would cause material harm (e.g., a production outage, a compliance deadline, or a safety issue).

## Deviation Procedure

1. Head of Product clearly documents why the standard timeline in SOP-PROD-012 cannot be met, including the business impact of waiting.
2. Head of Product contacts the VP Product directly (not only via MK-ProductHub) — by phone or in-person — to request verbal pre-approval.
3. On verbal pre-approval, Head of Product proceeds with the minimum necessary action to resolve the emergency, logging every action taken.
4. Head of Product files the formal request in MK-ProductHub within 24 hours of the verbal approval, marked 'Emergency — Retroactive Approval'.
5. The VP Product formally reviews and signs off on the retroactive request within 2 business days, confirming the emergency justification was reasonable.
6. If the VP Product finds the emergency justification insufficient, the case is escalated to Head of Product for a policy-compliance review.

## Important Note
