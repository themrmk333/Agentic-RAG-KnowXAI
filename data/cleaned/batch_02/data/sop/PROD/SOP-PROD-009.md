## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Product Manager logs into MK-ProductHub and navigates to the 'Pricing Change Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Product Manager selects 'New Pricing Change Request' and enters the required details, including revenue impact analysis and competitor pricing benchmark.

**Step 3:** the Product Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Product Manager submits the Pricing Change Request; MK-ProductHub automatically routes it to the VP Product for review.

**Step 5:** The VP Product reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-ProductHub updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Product Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Pricing Change Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-ProductHub for audit purposes. Pricing changes require Finance and Legal review before announcement.

The pricing change request is completed, logged in MK-ProductHub, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-ProductHub for future reference and audit.
