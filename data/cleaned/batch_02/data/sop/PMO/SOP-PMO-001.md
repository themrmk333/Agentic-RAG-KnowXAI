## Preconditions

- Employee ID and department

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Project Manager logs into MK-PMTrack and navigates to the 'Project Charter' section.

**Step 2:** the Project Manager selects 'New Project Charter' and enters the required details, including objectives, scope, budget estimate, and sponsor sign-off.

**Step 3:** the Project Manager attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Project Manager submits the Project Charter; MK-PMTrack automatically routes it to the PMO Director for review.

**Step 5:** The PMO Director reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-PMTrack updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Project Manager by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Project Charter is processed and the record is retained in MK-PMTrack for audit purposes. Projects without an approved charter cannot be resourced in MK-PMTrack.

## Expected Result

The project charter is completed, logged in MK-PMTrack, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-PMTrack for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Projects without an approved charter cannot be resourced in MK-PMTrack.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
