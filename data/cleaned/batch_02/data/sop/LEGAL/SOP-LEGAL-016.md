**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-LegalVault.

**Step 2:** the Legal Associate gathers the required data and documents, including verifying every executed contract is filed in MK-LegalVault with correct metadata.

**Step 5:** The Legal Manager discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-LegalVault with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Missing or misfiled contracts are corrected within the same audit cycle.
