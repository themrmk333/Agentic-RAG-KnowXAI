## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-Labs.

**Step 2:** the Research Lead gathers the required data and documents, including assessing bias, fairness, and potential misuse of the research direction.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Ethics Review against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The R&D Director discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-Labs with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Research involving sensitive use cases requires a documented ethics sign-off.

## Expected Result

The ethics review is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Research involving sensitive use cases requires a documented ethics sign-off.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
