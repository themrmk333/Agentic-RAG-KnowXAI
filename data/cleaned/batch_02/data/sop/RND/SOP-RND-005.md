## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **dataset acquisition and licensing procedure** within the Research & Development department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Research Associates and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Dataset Acquisition Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-Labs.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Research Associate**
- Approver: **Legal Manager**
- System of record: **MK-Labs**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-Labs.
- The dataset source, licence terms, and intended use must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the dataset acquisition request being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Research Associate logs into MK-Labs and navigates to the 'Dataset Acquisition Request' section.

**Step 2:** the Research Associate selects 'New Dataset Acquisition Request' and enters the required details, including the dataset source, licence terms, and intended use.

**Step 3:** the Research Associate attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Research Associate submits the Dataset Acquisition Request; MK-Labs automatically routes it to the Legal Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Legal Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-Labs updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Research Associate by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Dataset Acquisition Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-Labs for audit purposes. Datasets with unclear licensing are not used until Legal confirms permissibility.

## Expected Result

The dataset acquisition request is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Datasets with unclear licensing are not used until Legal confirms permissibility.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Research Associate → Research Lead → R&D Manager → R&D Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-RND-005 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
