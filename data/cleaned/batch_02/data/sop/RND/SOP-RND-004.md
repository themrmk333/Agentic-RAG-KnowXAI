## Preconditions

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the research handover being requested

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Research Lead logs into MK-Labs and navigates to the 'Research Handover' section.

**Step 2:** the Research Lead selects 'New Research Handover' and enters the required details, including model artifacts, evaluation results, and known limitations documentation.

**Step 3:** the Research Lead attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the Research Lead submits the Research Handover; MK-Labs automatically routes it to the Engineering Manager for review.

**Step 5:** The Engineering Manager reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-Labs updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the Research Lead by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Research Handover is processed and the record is retained in MK-Labs for audit purposes. Handovers without a documented limitations section are returned to R&D.

## Expected Result

The research handover is completed, logged in MK-Labs, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Labs for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Handovers without a documented limitations section are returned to R&D.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
