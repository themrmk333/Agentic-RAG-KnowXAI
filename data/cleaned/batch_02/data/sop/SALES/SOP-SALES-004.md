## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Account Executive confirms eligibility and prerequisites: pricing tier, contract term, and any custom clauses.

**Step 2:** the Account Executive raises a request in MK-CRM specifying exactly which Sales Quote is required.

**Step 3:** The Sales Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Sales Quote in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Account Executive is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-CRM and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-CRM marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Custom clauses require Legal review before the quote is sent.

## Expected Result

The sales quote is completed, logged in MK-CRM, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CRM for future reference and audit.
