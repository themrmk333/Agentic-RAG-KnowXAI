## Scenario
A genuine business emergency requires the email campaign to be actioned faster than the standard SOP allows, and waiting for the full standard approval chain would cause material harm (e.g., a production outage, a compliance deadline, or a safety issue).

## Deviation Procedure

1. Marketing Executive clearly documents why the standard timeline in SOP-MKT-010 cannot be met, including the business impact of waiting.
2. Marketing Executive contacts the Marketing Manager directly (not only via MK-MarketHub) — by phone or in-person — to request verbal pre-approval.
3. On verbal pre-approval, Marketing Executive proceeds with the minimum necessary action to resolve the emergency, logging every action taken.
4. Marketing Executive files the formal request in MK-MarketHub within 24 hours of the verbal approval, marked 'Emergency — Retroactive Approval'.
5. The Marketing Manager formally reviews and signs off on the retroactive request within 2 business days, confirming the emergency justification was reasonable.
6. If the Marketing Manager finds the emergency justification insufficient, the case is escalated to Marketing Director for a policy-compliance review.

## Important Note
