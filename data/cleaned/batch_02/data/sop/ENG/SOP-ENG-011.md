## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-CodeForge.

**Step 2:** the Software Engineer gathers the required data and documents, including architecture diagrams, data flow, and rollback/monitoring plan.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the Design Document against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The Tech Lead discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-CodeForge with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Designs impacting more than one service require a cross-team review meeting.

## Expected Result

The design document is completed, logged in MK-CodeForge, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CodeForge for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
