## Procedure

**Step 1:** the Finance Executive confirms eligibility and prerequisites: PAN/GST verification and bank account confirmation.

**Step 2:** the Finance Executive raises a request in MK-Finance360 specifying exactly which Vendor Master Record is required.

**Step 3:** The Finance Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 4:** Once approved, the assigned engineer provisions or configures the Vendor Master Record in the relevant system.

**Step 5:** the Finance Executive is notified and asked to test and verify the access or functionality.

**Step 6:** Any issues found during verification are logged in MK-Finance360 and corrected before sign-off.

**Step 7:** MK-Finance360 marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Vendors are not paid until the master record is fully verified.

## Expected Result

The vendor master record is completed, logged in MK-Finance360, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-Finance360 for future reference and audit.
