## Scenario
A genuine business emergency requires the vendor invoice to be actioned faster than the standard SOP allows, and waiting for the full standard approval chain would cause material harm (e.g., a production outage, a compliance deadline, or a safety issue).

1. Accountant clearly documents why the standard timeline in SOP-FIN-004 cannot be met, including the business impact of waiting.
2. Accountant contacts the Finance Lead directly (not only via MK-Finance360) — by phone or in-person — to request verbal pre-approval.

5. The Finance Lead formally reviews and signs off on the retroactive request within 2 business days, confirming the emergency justification was reasonable.
6. If the Finance Lead finds the emergency justification insufficient, the case is escalated to Finance Director for a policy-compliance review.
