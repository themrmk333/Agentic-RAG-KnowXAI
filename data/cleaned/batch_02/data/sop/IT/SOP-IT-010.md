## Procedure

**Step 1:** the IT Support Engineer confirms eligibility and prerequisites: the employee's full name, department, and manager for the distribution list.

**Step 3:** The IT Manager reviews and approves the request, applying least-privilege / need-basis principles.

**Step 7:** MK-ServiceDesk marks the request 'Complete' and the configuration is logged for audit and future reference. Shared mailboxes require an additional business owner to be named.
