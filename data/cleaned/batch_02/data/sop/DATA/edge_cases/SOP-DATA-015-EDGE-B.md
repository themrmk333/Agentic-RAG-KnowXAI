## Scenario
A genuine business emergency requires the analytics tool access to be actioned faster than the standard SOP allows, and waiting for the full standard approval chain would cause material harm (e.g., a production outage, a compliance deadline, or a safety issue).

1. requester clearly documents why the standard timeline in SOP-DATA-015 cannot be met, including the business impact of waiting.
2. requester contacts the Data Manager directly (not only via MK-DataLake) — by phone or in-person — to request verbal pre-approval.
3. On verbal pre-approval, requester proceeds with the minimum necessary action to resolve the emergency, logging every action taken.

5. The Data Manager formally reviews and signs off on the retroactive request within 2 business days, confirming the emergency justification was reasonable.
6. If the Data Manager finds the emergency justification insufficient, the case is escalated to Director of Data & Analytics for a policy-compliance review.
