## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **data onboarding for new team members** within the Data & Analytics department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Data Managers and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Data Onboarding** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-DataLake.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Data Manager**
- Approver: **Director of Data & Analytics**
- System of record: **MK-DataLake**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-DataLake.
- MK-DataLake access, SQL style guide, and a shadowing assignment must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the data onboarding being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-DataLake owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the Data Manager prepares the required Data Onboarding, including MK-DataLake access, SQL style guide, and a shadowing assignment.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The Director of Data & Analytics verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the Director of Data & Analytics for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-DataLake is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New hires complete a data-privacy module before touching production data.

## Expected Result

The data onboarding is completed, logged in MK-DataLake, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-DataLake for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- New hires complete a data-privacy module before touching production data.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Data Analyst → Analytics Lead → Data Manager → Director of Data & Analytics. Escalations should reference the SOP-DATA-011 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
