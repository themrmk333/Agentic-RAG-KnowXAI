## Purpose

This SOP defines the standard procedure for **customer satisfaction (csat) follow-up procedure** within the Customer Support / Helpdesk department at MK-INFINITE-AI PRIVATE LIMITED, ensuring the process is followed consistently, is auditable, and meets the department's service expectations.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all Support Team Leads and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **CSAT Review** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-SupportDesk.

## Applicable Roles

- Primary actor: **Support Team Lead**
- Approver: **Support Manager**
- System of record: **MK-SupportDesk**

## Preconditions

- The requester has an active MK-Infinite-AI account and access to MK-SupportDesk.
- Reviewing low CSAT scores and the associated ticket transcript must be available before starting this procedure.

## Required Information

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the csat review being requested
- Any supporting documentation required for approval

## Procedure

**Step 1:** The review is scheduled in advance and stakeholders are notified through MK-SupportDesk.

**Step 2:** the Support Team Lead gathers the required data and documents, including reviewing low CSAT scores and the associated ticket transcript.

**Step 3:** The review team examines the CSAT Review against the applicable policy or checklist.

**Step 4:** Gaps, risks, or non-conformities are documented with a severity rating and an owner assigned.

**Step 5:** The Support Manager discusses the findings with the relevant department and agrees remediation actions.

**Step 6:** Remediation actions are tracked in MK-SupportDesk with due dates and status.

**Step 7:** The review is closed once all actions are completed, and the outcome is recorded for future audits. Scores below 3/5 trigger a personal follow-up call from the Team Lead.

## Expected Result

The csat review is completed, logged in MK-SupportDesk, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-SupportDesk for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Scores below 3/5 trigger a personal follow-up call from the Team Lead.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.

## Escalation Procedure

If the request is not actioned within the expected timeframe, escalate in this order: Support Associate → Support Team Lead → Support Manager → Customer Experience Director. Escalations should reference the SOP-SUPPORT-007 document ID and the relevant ticket/request number.
