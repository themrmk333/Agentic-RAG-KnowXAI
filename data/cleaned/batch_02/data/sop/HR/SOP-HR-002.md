## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-HRMS owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the HR Executive prepares the required Onboarding Plan, including offer letter acknowledgment, document collection, and day-1 induction schedule.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The HR Manager verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the HR Manager for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-HRMS is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. Onboarding is considered complete only once all statutory documents are verified.

## Expected Result

The onboarding plan is completed, logged in MK-HRMS, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-HRMS for future reference and audit.
