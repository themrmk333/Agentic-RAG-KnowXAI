## Scenario
The designated approver (HR Director) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the hr team onboarding cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. HR Manager confirms the HR Director has an active out-of-office status in MK-HRMS or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. HR Manager identifies the HR Director's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (HR Executive -> HR Business Partner -> HR Manager -> HR Director) — as recorded in MK-HRMS.
3. HR Manager resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-HRMS, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original HR Director would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the HR Director returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-HRMS logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to HR Onboarding for New HR Team Members decisions explicitly reserved for the HR Director by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the HR Director's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: HR Executive -> HR Business Partner -> HR Manager -> HR Director.
