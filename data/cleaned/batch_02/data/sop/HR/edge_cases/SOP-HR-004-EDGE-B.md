## Scenario
A genuine business emergency requires the performance review to be actioned faster than the standard SOP allows, and waiting for the full standard approval chain would cause material harm (e.g., a production outage, a compliance deadline, or a safety issue).

## Deviation Procedure

1. Reporting Manager clearly documents why the standard timeline in SOP-HR-004 cannot be met, including the business impact of waiting.
2. Reporting Manager contacts the HR Business Partner directly (not only via MK-HRMS) — by phone or in-person — to request verbal pre-approval.
3. On verbal pre-approval, Reporting Manager proceeds with the minimum necessary action to resolve the emergency, logging every action taken.
4. Reporting Manager files the formal request in MK-HRMS within 24 hours of the verbal approval, marked 'Emergency — Retroactive Approval'.
5. The HR Business Partner formally reviews and signs off on the retroactive request within 2 business days, confirming the emergency justification was reasonable.
6. If the HR Business Partner finds the emergency justification insufficient, the case is escalated to HR Director for a policy-compliance review.
