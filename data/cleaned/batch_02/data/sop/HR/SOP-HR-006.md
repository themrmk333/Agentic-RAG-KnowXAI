This procedure applies to all employees and any employee across MK-Infinite-AI who needs to initiate a **Transfer Request** request. It covers the full workflow from initiation through to closure in MK-HRMS.

- Employee ID and department
- Details of the transfer request being requested

## Procedure

**Step 1:** the employee logs into MK-HRMS and navigates to the 'Transfer Request' section.

**Step 2:** the employee selects 'New Transfer Request' and enters the required details, including current manager no-objection and target team acceptance.

**Step 3:** the employee attaches any supporting documentation and reviews the entry for accuracy before submitting.

**Step 4:** the employee submits the Transfer Request; MK-HRMS automatically routes it to the HR Business Partner for review.

**Step 5:** The HR Business Partner reviews the request against company policy and either approves, rejects, or returns it for clarification.

**Step 6:** On approval, MK-HRMS updates the status to 'Approved' and notifies the employee by email/in-app notification.

**Step 7:** The approved Transfer Request is processed and the record is retained in MK-HRMS for audit purposes. Transfers within probation period require additional HR review.

## Expected Result

The transfer request is completed, logged in MK-HRMS, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-HRMS for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- Transfers within probation period require additional HR review.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
