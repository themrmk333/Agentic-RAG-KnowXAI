## Scenario
The designated approver (Procurement Director) is on leave, travel, or otherwise unreachable within the normal response window, but the sourcing review cannot wait for their return.

## Deviation Procedure

1. Procurement Specialist confirms the Procurement Director has an active out-of-office status in MK-ProcureX or MK-HRMS before invoking this path.
2. Procurement Specialist identifies the Procurement Director's designated delegate — typically the next role up the escalation chain (Procurement Executive -> Procurement Specialist -> Procurement Manager -> Procurement Director) — as recorded in MK-ProcureX.
3. Procurement Specialist resubmits or flags the existing request in MK-ProcureX, tagging it 'Delegate Approval Required' with the reason.
4. The delegate reviews the request using the same criteria the original Procurement Director would apply, and either approves, rejects, or requests clarification.
5. Once the Procurement Director returns, they are notified of the delegate decision for their records; they may flag it for a follow-up review within 5 business days if something looks inconsistent with policy.
6. MK-ProcureX logs both the delegate's identity and the reason for delegation on the request record for audit purposes.

## Important Note
Delegation does not apply to Sustainable/Ethical Sourcing Review decisions explicitly reserved for the Procurement Director by policy (e.g., anything requiring named signing authority); those must wait for the Procurement Director's return or be routed to the next level up, not a lateral delegate.

## Escalation
If this edge case procedure itself cannot be completed, escalate through: Procurement Executive -> Procurement Specialist -> Procurement Manager -> Procurement Director.
