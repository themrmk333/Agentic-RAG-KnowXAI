## Procedure

**Step 1:** HR notifies the relevant department and the MK-CloudOps owner ahead of the employee's start or end date.

**Step 2:** the DevOps Manager prepares the required DevOps Onboarding Plan, including cloud console access, IaC repo access, and an on-call shadowing schedule.

**Step 3:** Access, equipment, or accounts are provisioned (onboarding) or scheduled for deactivation (offboarding).

**Step 4:** The DevOps Director verifies that every item on the checklist is complete.

**Step 5:** The employee, or their manager for offboarding cases, confirms receipt or completion.

**Step 6:** Any exceptions or missing items are escalated to the DevOps Director for same-day resolution.

**Step 7:** MK-CloudOps is updated to reflect the completed status and the record is archived. New DevOps engineers do not join the on-call rotation until week 3.

## Expected Result

The devops onboarding plan is completed, logged in MK-CloudOps, and the requester is notified of the outcome. The record remains available in MK-CloudOps for future reference and audit.

## Exceptions

- New DevOps engineers do not join the on-call rotation until week 3.
- Requests that do not meet the preconditions above are returned to the requester with an explanation rather than being rejected outright.
